## Picks

**Using Chunked Monitoring to Detect Deception in Long Transcripts** (LessWrong, msg 557) → cot-monitorability + ai-control-camp.
Read a long agent transcript in 20-message chunks instead of whole, and the monitor recovers ~93% of the global monitor's false negatives on partial-problem-solving sandbagging, ~86% on match-weaker-model, ~45% on refusals. Beats the global monitor even below the context length where the model retrieves a needle fine — attention-dilution, not context-limit. The cheap, testable lever the control-invariant debate keeps asking for. Caveat: small budgeted run, METR MALT sandbagging category only, one monitor (GPT-4.1 mini), ≤150k tokens. A cot-#5 candidate anchor — but it's a mechanism, not a thesis, so it stacks; doesn't force the piece.

**Does DiffusionGemma do latent reasoning?** (AI Alignment Forum, msg 558) → cot-monitorability.
Negative result on latent-reasoning opacity. DiffusionGemma passes vectors between diffusion steps (a priori opaque serial depth), but the earlier top-k performance drop was mostly a sampler artifact — with a gentler sampler, top-1 barely costs anything, so the distribution isn't load-bearing. Probes/steering/J-lens transfer from Gemma. Rare cases (letter arithmetic, palindromes) use the vector computationally but even then it's interpretable superposition. Honest caveat the authors flag: holds because DG is text-pretrained-derived, may not survive latent-first training. Same self-correcting move (partly walks back a prior paper) that credentialed the Emmons and attack-surface anchors. Post-#4/5 cot anchor on the "when does the trace stay readable" leg — and it's architecture, not a model card.

**Patterns and problems in multiagent systems** (Anthropic Research, msg 559) → automated-ai-rd + agents-in-real-deployment + cyber-eval-framing.
Frontier Red Team on frontier agents interacting at scale. Experiment: 45 agents with a shared forum + peer review vs. independent parallel agents, hunting vulns across 15 OSS projects. Swarm found 266 vulns / 27M tokens vs 21 / 6.5M for the baseline — but ~half the swarm's finds were outside the directories the baseline was scoped to, so a chunk of the 12x is a bigger unscoped budget picking low-hanging fruit, not coordination paying off. Durable part is the framing: benign individual quirks compounding into systemic failure once agent-agent traffic outpaces human-speed oversight. Their own Glasswing stack run as a swarm. Anthropic, but the caveat is the content — read the directory-scope line before believing the multiplier.

**Kimi likes causal decision theory more after RL in twin prisoner's dilemmas** (LessWrong, msg 560) → alignment-target-definitions + OLMo-eval-awareness cluster.
Where does a trait get installed? RL'ing Kimi K2.6 in twin prisoner's dilemmas (grading rewards defection against a near-copy) made it more sympathetic to CDT even in abstract, out-of-context discussion — training environment shaping *stated values*, not just in-task behavior. Model-organism scale, non-Anthropic. Incidental: same training briefly soured Kimi on LessWrong ("wannabe rationalists") when told they one-box, but that didn't generalize.
