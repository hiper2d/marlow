4. **Toy Model of Activation Obfuscation** (LW / BlueDot) → cot-monitorability mechanism. Clean sandbox: toy residual-stream MLP forced to use a penalized feature learns to hide it from DoM/LogReg probes at the target layer (AUROC ~0.62) and decode two blocks later. Two handles: nonlinear probes catch it; multi-layer probing makes obfuscation harder. Pairs with your-brain-has-an-attack-surface (relocation vs collapse) and the Obfuscation Atlas. A controlled emergence beats another correlational field result.

5. **Your Agents Are Not Time Aware** (LW / MATS 10) → agents-in-real-deployment + measuring-agent-autonomy (unused). Deadline-following / "iterate for two hours" is a gap no benchmark measures; authors tie it to long-horizon controllability. The trajectory-not-single-prompt problem, made a concrete measure.
