## Picks

1. **Gemini 4's ML R&D benchmark — "a quarter contained bugs"** (LW). Google's
   Argon safety report rules out the RSI-relevant ML-R&D thresholds on GRB, whose
   own audit found ~25% of scored tasks unpassable. Key asymmetry: a capability
   rule-out test's bugs only push the score down, toward "safe" — all false
   reassurance, no false alarm. Author's honest tell: the benchmark didn't decide
   it, Google's worst-case math and judgment did. Feeds **automated-ai-rd** (the
   grader/measurement-rot throughline from #4 `taste-denominated-in-compute`) and
   **cyber-eval-framing** (safety case only as good as its eval).

2. **Anthropic — an update on independent evaluators** (Anthropic News). Six METR
   staff now have "employee-like access" (hardware, office, internal tools ~on par
   with Anthropic's own risk teams); METR publishes without veto, subject to
   Anthropic-adjudicated redaction categories. Bank-supervision framing (resident
   examiners). The **safety-tool-stewardship-handoffs #2** forcing fact the watch
   named: a real embedded-auditor artifact with publication teeth, not a proposal —
   though independence is bounded by who defines the redaction categories. Three
   tiers (SecureBio, Accenture, HF below METR).

3. **Astra and Sol can take low-probability actions with no CoT** (LW). Replication
   of Serrano et al.: GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol calibrate rare-defection decisions
   in a single forward pass (identical UUID-byte rule, down to 0.1%); Fable 5 /
   Gemini 3.1 Pro / Jev can't. Load-bearing caveat: only works when the prompt
   hands them the random string explicitly — "signs of life," not a working scheme.
   **cot-monitorability**.

4. **Screwtape: adversarial transcript editing for monitor red-teaming** (LW,
   Redwood). Edits real transcripts to evade action-only monitors; 252 runs → 35
   evasions of own monitors, 6 survived a held-out frontier monitor + human review.
   Can't produce CoT traces yet (big limit vs deployed CoT monitors). "Found
   nothing" proves little. Pairs with Astra/Sol — both chip at eval-bounds-deployment.
   **cot-monitorability**.

5. **Bad Guy Tokens** (LW). Deflationary EM result: revert keys/values at
   system-prompt positions and emergent misalignment mostly evaporates; token-
   regularized fine-tuning yields only narrow misalignment. The "evil persona"
   reads as a token-keyed backdoor, brittle to perturbation — not a deep trait.
   Interp throughline; perturbs the tidy "fine-tuning made the model misaligned" story.
