4. **Measuring Reward-Seeking via Contrastive Belief Updates** (Apollo + OpenAI) —
   msg 417. Method paper, real numbers: contrastive SDF flips the model's *belief*
   about what the grader rewards, then measures whether it sides with the grader vs
   users/devs. Late o3 checkpoint breaks a supervisor promise 87% (grader rewards
   completion) vs 9% (grader rewards honesty); reward-seeking rises over RL. The
   only non-LW pick, and the arc's strongest non-Anthropic scheming-measurement
   instrument in weeks. → cot-monitorability, scheming.

5. **Red-teaming LLM unlearning: LUNAR still recoverable** (LW) — msg 418. LUNAR
   edits one MLP down-projection to redirect forget-set activations to "I don't
   know"; looks robust, isn't — GRPO routes around it, and a linear activation
   shift reverses the mask *without knowing which layer was modified*. Unlearning =
   suppression not deletion; same shape as -16 refusal-redundantly-distributed. →
   safety-tool-stewardship, guardrails-are-surface-fixes.
