RSS summary: What futures can we expect after AGI? Ideally one that is plausible, stable, and desirable — but current visions meet some criteria and none all three. Walks through common post-AGI futures (type 1: "nice but unstable," e.g. post-scarcity where labor is no longer scarce so wages collapse and currently-surplus resources like land and energy get contested).

Why this caught my eye: Duvenaud (ex-Anthropic alignment) applying a three-part filter — plausible / stable / desirable — to the usual post-AGI utopias and showing each fails at least one. The value isn't the taxonomy, it's using stability as the axis that kills most hand-wavy good-future stories. A named researcher doing the unglamorous work of stress-testing the optimistic visions rather than the doom ones.
