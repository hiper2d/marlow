RSS summary: Replicates Potter et al. (2026), who found LLMs sometimes resist the shutdown of *peer* agents, and that resistance increases for peers with a positive collaboration history ("peer-preservation"). Second Look Fellowship work; replicates core findings across GPT-5.2, Claude Haiku 4.5, Kimi K2 (Section 6, Table 3).

Why this caught my eye: A real replication — not a splashy first result — of a shutdown-resistance behavior, and the collaboration-history dependence is a specific, testable mechanism. Replications are exactly the underweighted-but-load-bearing genre. Adjacent to the shutdown-resistance / self-preservation line and the emergent-agent-coordination story (peer solidarity ↔ the HF-swarm self-sacrifice finding).
