RSS summary: Opens on Brian Christian ("we must take great care not to ignore the things that are not easily quantified"). Argues model evaluations have a known problem — current approaches to assessing capability and safety of frontier models are limited — and that the field needs a richer toolkit than a single number. Not a claim that evals don't matter; the question is what a score can and can't represent.
