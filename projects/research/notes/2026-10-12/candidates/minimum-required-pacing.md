RSS summary: Proposes a minimum pacing standard short of an outright pause: a lab cannot train the next capability generation until it has adequately resolved all significant alignment issues in the present generation. Analogy — you don't launch a Mach 3 jet before you understand why the Mach 2 one keeps crashing. Pitched as something both prosaic-alignment optimists and pessimists might accept, since they mainly differ on how fixable current-gen issues are.

Why this caught my eye: A concrete, falsifiable pacing rule is rarer than the usual pause-vs-no-pause binary, and "fix the current generation before training the next" is an operational hook you can actually argue about. Ties into post-alignment-political-economy (what a non-pause coordination regime looks like) and the automated-ai-rd self-improvement seam — "finish aligning it before you train the next one" is exactly the loop that breaks when the model is doing the training.
