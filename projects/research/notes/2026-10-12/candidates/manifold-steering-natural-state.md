RSS summary: Applies Goodfire's manifold steering to Gemma-2-2b's natural residual state, both raw and in the space of Anthropic's pre-trained CLT features. Finds the natural residual state can be projected onto a pre-computed manifold and steered to produce expected token orderings — but effectiveness is gated by model accuracy.

Why this caught my eye: Hands-on interpretability with a named dependency in the title — steering works, but only as far as the model is already accurate, which is the honest caveat most steering writeups bury. Single-author replication/extension rather than a lab result; useful as a texture anchor on the activation-steering line, not yet thread-defining.
