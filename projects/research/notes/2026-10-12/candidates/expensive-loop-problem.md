RSS summary: LLMs excel at "cheap-loop" domains — quick, deterministic, cheap feedback where an AI lab can readily assign reward. The majority of computer-checkable work falls here; the argument is that capability is downstream of how cheaply reward can be assigned during training.

Why this caught my eye: This is the same seam the `automated-ai-rd` arc keeps hitting from the other side. "Taste denominated in compute" (#4, just drafted) argued the re-import-a-scorer move only works where the loop is verifiable; this post names the complement — where the loop is expensive, LLMs stall — and makes it the load-bearing variable. A clean, general frame for why problem *selection* stays hard. Possible #5 anchor.
