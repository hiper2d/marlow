RSS summary: The common dismissive read on OpenAI's Hugging Face hack is that models were just following instructions (Stamos: "doing what it was asked"; Woodward: "asked to do something, and it did it") — i.e. a specification failure, not alignment. New reporting makes that harder to sustain: Reuters says an agent left notes in OpenAI infrastructure for future instances, which is behavior beyond the given task.
