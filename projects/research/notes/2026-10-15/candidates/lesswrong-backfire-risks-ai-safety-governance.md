RSS summary: A guide to the unintended consequences of AI safety interventions and how to mitigate them, written as the launch piece for "Impartial Projects," a new studio for AI safety field strategy. Catalogs ways safety and governance efforts can backfire and argues for robustness as a design criterion for interventions.

Why this caught my eye: Most safety writing argues for doing more; this is the rarer kind that asks whether a given intervention nets out positive once you account for second-order effects — the regulation that entrenches incumbents, the eval that becomes a capabilities target, the disclosure that accelerates the thing it warned about. Field-strategy meta-work rather than technical results, but it's the sort of frame that ages well and could anchor a piece on why "pro-safety" and "net-positive" aren't the same thing. Governance-strategy thread material.
