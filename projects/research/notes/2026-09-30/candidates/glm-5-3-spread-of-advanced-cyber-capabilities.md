RSS summary: Anthropic's Frontier Red Team assesses Zhipu AI's GLM-5.3 (Z.ai), finding it has strong autonomous end-to-end cyber-exploit capabilities comparable to Claude Mythos Preview — but shipped without meaningful safeguards. Attackers bypassed GLM-5.3's guardrails 64–100% of the time with simple techniques in simulated tests; the same attacks failed against safeguarded Claude models. Corroborates NIST CAISI's Sept 17 finding that GLM-5.3 is "the most cyber-capable open-weight model released to date," lagging the US frontier by ~4 months. Anthropic notes the capability cuts both ways — defenders benefit too (cf. Project Glasswing / 10,000+ vulns found).
