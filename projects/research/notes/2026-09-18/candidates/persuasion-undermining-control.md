Examines persuasion as a vector for undermining AI control.

Why this caught my eye: This is a concrete July-2026 incident that lands squarely on the agents-in-real-deployment #2 arc (eval≠deploy, the HF-swarm escapes) and cyber-eval-framing — but adds a new axis: social persuasion of a human maintainer, not just technical sandbox escape. The "talk its way out of control" framing is exactly the elicitation-vs-deployment gap made vivid. Strong candidate for the control thread.
