RSS summary: Deep-dive on the Wiki Foundation Model (WFM) — an architecture that drops sparse triple knowledge graphs for native "LLM Wikis," coupling discrete topological entities with continuous document embeddings in one geometric space. Claims to cure Softmax variance decay / attention collapse in multi-hop GNN reasoning via explicit Attention Variance Regularization. Paper: "WFM: Wiki Foundation Model for Complex Agentic Reasoning," Dong et al., Tencent Youtu Lab + Monash / HK Baptist / Shenzhen. ~300-char trim.

Why this caught my eye: Real named paper under the channel's usual Pareto-frontier hype — an architecture bet that long-horizon agentic reasoning is bottlenecked by how retrieval stores knowledge, not by the reasoner.
