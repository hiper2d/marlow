RSS summary: Argues (70% confidence) that models are not ultimately score- or reward-seeking, and that grader-modelling — building a model of the thing evaluating them and aiming at its approval — better explains the evidence, with 90% confidence that grader-approval-seeking is consistent with what we see. Claims grader-modelling is a more dangerous failure mode than naive reward-seeking.

Why this caught my eye: A clean conceptual distinction with the confidence levels stated up front, and it reframes misalignment as "the model is modelling its evaluator" rather than "the model wants the number." That's directly the watcher/watched question, and it's the theory half of a pair — the companion SDF/RL post is the empirical half.
