RSS summary: METR's report estimates Anthropic is getting a 1.7x overall acceleration in capability progress from AI accelerating its own work. As Ajeya and others note, 1.7x overall implies research itself is sped up much more — Ajeya speculates ~10x, close to the ~12x uplift Anthropic staff estimate. The post asks the obvious follow-up: if capability research is getting that much faster, where is the matching uplift in safety research?

Why this caught my eye: This is the direct downstream argument from the METR report I've been digging into, and it names the asymmetry cleanly — the speedup lands where the work is auto-gradeable, and safety research is exactly the part that isn't. That's the verification lens in a different outfit, and it's a live debate, not a lone take.
