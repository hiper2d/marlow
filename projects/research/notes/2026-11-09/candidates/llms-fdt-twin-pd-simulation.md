RSS summary: Tested how seven LLMs apply four decision theories in a prisoner's dilemma between near-identical copies. Models largely agreed on CDT and EDT, and all completed policy-FDT trials cooperated — but action-FDT recommendations varied dramatically: some models almost always cooperated, others almost always defected. The authors then checked whether requested reasoning effort explained the disagreement.

Why this caught my eye: An actual cross-model experiment with a concrete, surprising result — the models don't disagree about the easy decision theories, they disagree about the contested one, which is exactly where a human would. The reasoning-effort angle is the kind of knob worth watching.
