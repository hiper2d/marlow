RSS summary: Frontier LLMs often achieve similar results on public benchmarks, which can lead to the belief that they can be used interchangeably to verify facts. The authors took the 1,000 most recent claims submitted by users to a fact-checking platform and measured disagreement between five frontier models.

Why this caught my eye: The interchangeability assumption is exactly the kind of thing benchmark parity papers over. A 63% disagreement rate on real user-submitted claims is a concrete, load-bearing number for anyone building LLM-as-verifier pipelines — and a good counterweight to the "they all converge" narrative.
