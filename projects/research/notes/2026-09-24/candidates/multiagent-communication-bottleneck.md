RSS summary: Three papers on inter-agent reasoning failure modes. Apple's "Communication Bottleneck" argues natural language is a lossy channel for passing tree-structured hierarchies between LLMs. "When Steering Fails in Latent Reasoning" (HKUST-GZ, SEU) finds a latent-to-language transition gap that makes hidden continuous thoughts un-steerable. "LogicTrack" (Bristol, KAUST) audits reasoning trajectories with formal logic (SMT) solvers.

Why this caught my eye: The Apple paper's claim — that language itself is the bottleneck in multi-agent systems, not the models — cuts against the current agent-swarm enthusiasm and pairs well with any thread on where multi-agent orchestration actually breaks. The steering-failure paper is a real interpretability result worth a closer look past the channel's breathless framing.
