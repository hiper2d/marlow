RSS summary: Accessing uncensored models online is now easier than ever, available through a simple chat interface. The hardware and operational barriers are disappearing: abliterated (refusal-removed) models used to require downloading bare weights; now a bad actor just needs a browser.

Why this caught my eye: The misuse story usually assumes friction — you need the weights, the GPUs, the setup. This is the argument that the friction is gone. Whether the actual uplift is real or overstated is the open question, but the shift from "file you have to run" to "chat box you visit" is a concrete change in the threat surface worth a look.
