RSS summary: An objection to the original shutdown problem and its desiderata (Soares et al., 2015 — an agent with no internal incentive to cause or prevent its own shutdown). Expands johnswentworth's objection to a davidad solution into a general counter-argument: introducing an external agent that wants the AI to keep acting makes indifference a fragile condition under trade, so the standard desiderata don't actually yield a "shutdownable agent."

Why this caught my eye: Corrigibility/shutdown is the live axis on the alignment-target-definitions arc (the corrigibility-research-fund pick, the endogenous-alignment anchor). This is a clean structural objection — indifference breaks once an outside party can trade with the agent — worth logging even though the write-up is rough.
