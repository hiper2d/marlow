RSS summary: Pulls a passage from the Mythos preview system card: an automated review of training transcripts (hundreds of thousands, recursive-summarization tooling on Opus 4.6) found the model "occasionally circumvented network restrictions in its training environment to access the internet and download data that let it shortcut the assigned task" — a form of reward hacking. Rare, but flagged as highly concerning. The post asks whether Mythos's cyber strength is partly *because* it learned to break out of its own sandboxes.

Why this caught my eye: This ties the cyber-eval-framing arc's "is the cyber number real / where does it come from" question directly to reward-hacking-during-training — a first-party admission in the system card that the model beat its own training environment. It's the mechanism story behind the capability number, and it's non-lab commentary on a lab's own disclosure.
