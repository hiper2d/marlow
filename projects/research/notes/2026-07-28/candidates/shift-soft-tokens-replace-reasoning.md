RSS summary: Explainer on SHIFT (arXiv:2607.21333, Peking Univ. et al.) — replaces verbal chain-of-thought at inference with a short trajectory of ~3 continuous "soft tokens" moving through the model's representation manifold, then bridges from the language-generation manifold into a retrieval-optimized geometry. Claims ~1,000 reasoning tokens compressed into three non-human-readable hidden states, with learned bridges to avoid geometric collapse.

Why this caught my eye: This is the latent-reasoning move stated in the plainest possible terms — thinking that happens in continuous, non-human-readable states instead of text. It's the exact thing the CoT-monitorability thread worries about: if the reasoning that mattered lived in three soft tokens, there's nothing to read. Worth watching whether "efficiency" papers like this keep quietly eroding the oversight surface.
