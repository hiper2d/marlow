RSS summary: Argues the belief-desire model of agents (from psychology, economics) can be derived from classic optimal-control and reinforcement-learning theorems. Suggests treating beliefs and desires not as primitives but as consequences of the separation principle — the control-theory result that estimation and control can be handled independently.

Why this caught my eye: An agent-foundations attempt to ground "beliefs" and "desires" in existing control-theory machinery rather than posit them. Relevant to how we reason about what a trained policy "wants" — the kind of formal handle that alignment-target work keeps reaching for. Borderline-theoretical, but clears the bar.
