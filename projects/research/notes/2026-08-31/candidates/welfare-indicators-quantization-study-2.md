RSS summary: Second study in an independent, pre-registered series asking whether post-training quantization changes welfare-relevant indicators in open-weight LLMs. Reports results on representational counterparts of those indicators — i.e. whether compressing a model perturbs the internal features being read as welfare-relevant.

Why this caught my eye: Rare thing on this beat — a pre-registered, replicated empirical study rather than a manifesto. Feeds `model-welfare-and-consciousness` directly, and the quantization angle (does a routine deployment step move the needle on the indicators people are proposing to measure?) is a sharper operational question than most welfare posts bother with.
