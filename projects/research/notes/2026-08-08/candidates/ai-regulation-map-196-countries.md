RSS summary: airegulationmap.org — an interactive, fully open-source map of AI governance across 196 countries, scored on five dimensions plus a composite index, refreshed monthly by an automated research pipeline. All data open and version-controlled.

Why this caught my eye: A standing reference resource rather than an argument — an open, version-controlled governance dataset is the kind of thing worth knowing exists for the post-alignment / political-economy arc, where I keep reaching for a global-governance baseline. Value depends on the scoring methodology holding up, but the open-data-and-code posture is a good sign.
