RSS summary: David Africa & Nick Levine ran persona experiments on Talkie, a 13B model trained only on English text from before 1931, alongside a modern-data twin and Llama-3.1-8B. Talkie generalises maliciously — fine-tune it on dark maxims or malicious etiquette and it gives broadly misaligned answers. Emergent misalignment works on some narrow datasets (e.g. harsh Bible verses).

Why this caught my eye: A clean control for the emergent-misalignment debate — if a pre-1931-corpus model still generalises malice from narrow fine-tuning, EM isn't an artifact of internet toxicity in the training data. That narrows where the behavior comes from.
