RSS summary: LASR Labs Summer 2026 research (supervised by Bartosz Cywinski). Fine-tuning a model on something it internally represents as false can make it broadly misaligned, even when the training data contains nothing harmful. The effect shows up when a model is pushed to say what it still believes to be false.

Why this caught my eye: This is the emergent-misalignment line of work with a sharper handle — not "train on bad data, get bad model," but "train on things the model knows are false, get broad misalignment from clean data." That's a more disturbing and more specific claim, and it connects to the honesty/introspection threads.
