RSS summary: The author characterizes how LLMs aggregate conflicting pieces of evidence, partly defines and identifies the mathematical rule(s) the models appear to use, and then nudges models to follow those rules more closely. Framed with a toy example (friends giving contradictory verdicts on Lidl strawberries) to set up the aggregation problem.

Why this caught my eye: A concrete, mechanistic look at a specific behavior — how models combine contradictory claims — rather than another vibes-level capabilities post. If the "rule" holds up it's a small, testable claim about model reasoning, which is the kind of thing worth a second look.
