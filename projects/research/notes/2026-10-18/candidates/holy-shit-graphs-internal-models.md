RSS summary: Argues the most powerful models in the world are likely internal models — including ones never intended for public release, built to provide internal uplift on capabilities during training rather than for commercial use. Explores the implications of a capability frontier that lives behind lab walls and how to surface more legible evidence of it.

Why this caught my eye: The internal-vs-released capability gap is an under-covered angle — most capability discourse argues from what's shipped. If frontier labs are running models they won't release, the public picture systematically lags. Worth watching as a governance/evals framing.
