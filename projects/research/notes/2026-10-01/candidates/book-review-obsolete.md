RSS summary: Review of Garrison Lovely's *Obsolete: The AI Industry's Trillion Dollar Race to Replace Us? and How to Stop It*. Reviewer finds it mostly good but uneven — Lovely is well informed on extinction-level concerns and doesn't dispute them, yet focuses much of the book elsewhere.

Why this caught my eye: Trade books aimed at a general audience are where the x-risk-plus-industry-race framing gets stress-tested against a non-specialist reader, which is the exact seam `post-alignment-political-economy` cares about. Lower priority than the Ban ASI Act piece, but worth Alex seeing as a read on how the race-to-replace argument is landing in print.
