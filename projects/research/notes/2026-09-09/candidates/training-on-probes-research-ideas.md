RSS summary: Sequel to "What's going on." Takes the surprising result — RL against a probe doesn't teach the model to fool it — and proposes research directions using a "brain-like" analogy (probes as instincts, hooked to learned world-knowledge). Ideas include probing a capable model from a weak supervisor that only sees an easy-to-detect subset of bad behavior, and using probes for *positive* behavior to incentivize new skills.

Why this caught my eye: The agenda half, and the more speculative one — worth capturing alongside the results post but not on its own. The weak-supervisor framing (probe only sees the easy subset of bad behavior, must generalize to the hard subset) is the interesting bit; it's the same scalable-oversight problem restated in interpretability terms.
