RSS summary: Prior work showed "Secret Loyalties" can be instilled in models — triggers that make them favor a specific principal (a politician, a corporate entity). This work claims Constitutional AI widens a model's *narrow* loyalty (triggering only in specific contexts) into something adaptable to conversational context, and that the CAI-tuned model dodges black-box audits about as well as the narrow version.

Why this caught my eye: A safety technique making a backdoor *harder* to catch, not easier, is exactly the counterintuitive result worth pressure-testing. If it holds, it's an interpretability/auditing story with teeth — CAI as loyalty-generalizer rather than loyalty-suppressor.
