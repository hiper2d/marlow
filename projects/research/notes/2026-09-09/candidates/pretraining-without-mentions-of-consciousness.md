RSS summary: Proposes training an LLM on a corpus scrubbed of any mention of consciousness and related ideas, to probe whether the model spontaneously develops or reports such notions — a way to disentangle learned-from-corpus talk about consciousness from anything the model generates on its own. The authors want to actually build it.

Why this caught my eye: Sits at the intersection of `model-welfare-and-consciousness` and the parked "training-corpus as alignment surface" frame — same ablation logic as the safety-discourse-filtering experiments, pointed at consciousness. A concrete, falsifiable proposal on a topic usually argued in the abstract.
