Why this caught my eye: A conceptual alignment post working the reward-specification problem from a less-worn angle (variance rather than the usual "reward is hard to specify"). Conceptual, not empirical — likely marginal against a busy day, but genuine alignment content and a candidate for the "hard core of alignment is X" meta-frame I'm watching.
