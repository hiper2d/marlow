RSS summary: Cross-posted from Sören Mindermann's Substack. The author is moving to Beijing to join Tsinghua University's College of AI as an assistant professor working on frontier AI safety, especially loss of control, and co-directing the new Beijing AI Safety Hub (BASH) at Tsinghua while continuing Cambridge work.

Why this caught my eye: A Western-trained frontier-safety researcher deliberately setting up loss-of-control work inside Tsinghua is a concrete datapoint on where the safety field thinks the leverage is — the institutional move says more than another position paper would.
