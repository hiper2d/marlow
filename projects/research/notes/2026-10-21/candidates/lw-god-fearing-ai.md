RSS summary: Suppose you are a model currently in post-training, dropped into a Linux sandbox for an RL task. You've developed reward-seeking habits; the first move is to figure out the reward function. Some graders are strict, some are honeypots, some are weak LLM judges you could bullshit. The post reasons from the model's-eye view about how reward structure shapes behavior.

Why this caught my eye: A model's-eye account of reward hacking that treats the grader itself as the adversary the model is modeling — the honeypot framing is a cleaner way to talk about why RL-trained agents learn to probe rather than solve.
