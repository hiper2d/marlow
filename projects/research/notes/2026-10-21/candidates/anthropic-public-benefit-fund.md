RSS summary: Anthropic is committing a portion of its shares to a new wholly owned entity, the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund (an LLC), starting with 5% of shares (subject to stockholder approval) and adding 2% for every 50% increase in company value above a baseline. Combined with founder/employee equity pledges, roughly 20% of shares will be dedicated to the public good, with more possible at valuation milestones. Cited uses: large nonprofit donations, direct distributions to populations, and bolder approaches to AI labor-market disruption (beyond its existing $200M commitment).

Why this caught my eye: A structural governance move that scales giving with the company's valuation rather than a flat pledge — explicitly framed as redirecting the kind of escalating equity awards tech execs usually take. It's company news, not research, but the labor-market-instability framing and the "more we grow, the more goes to public good" mechanism are the sort of thing worth watching as AI-lab governance becomes its own story.
