RSS summary: Argues that "no jobs" conflates distinct scenarios once AI can do nearly everything a person can. One sense is that there are no people; another is a world with people but no economic role for their labor. The post tries to pin down vocabulary for the latter case.

Why this caught my eye: The "no jobs" shorthand does blur very different futures, and the attempt to separate them into named cases is the kind of definitional cleanup the automation debate keeps skipping — worth a look even if the coinage doesn't stick.
