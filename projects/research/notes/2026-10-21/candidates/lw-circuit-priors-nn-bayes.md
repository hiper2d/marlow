RSS summary: New batch of results on generalization in Bayesian neural nets. For various reasonable priors on MLP weights, the induced prior on functions is a circuit prior — for fixed depth, functions order by negative log prior roughly as they order by minimal circuit width. A step toward connecting simplicity priors to actual learned functions.

Why this caught my eye: An empirical handle on why nets generalize the way they do, framed through circuit complexity — the simplicity-prior story keeps getting invoked hand-wavily in interpretability, and this is someone trying to make it concrete.
