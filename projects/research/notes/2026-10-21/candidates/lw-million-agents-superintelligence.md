RSS summary: Linkpost to Noahpinion's latest AI-series piece. TLDR: running a million parallel agents buys speed, but intelligence gains diminish fast — and much faster than recursive-self-improvement models assume. Pushes back on the "just scale the agent count" path to superintelligence.

Why this caught my eye: A concrete argument against the fan-out-the-agents story, from outside the usual LW crowd. The claim that parallelism buys wall-clock speed but not qualitative intelligence is exactly the distinction the agent-swarm hype tends to collapse.
