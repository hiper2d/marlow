RSS summary: Post-training walkthrough — SFT, RL, DPO, and the newer on-policy distillation variants (OPD, OPSD). Anchors on three concurrent papers: On-Policy Self-Distillation (Meta Superintelligence Labs / UCLA / HKU, arXiv:2601.18734), Tsinghua's "Rethinking On-Policy Distillation" (phenomenology, mechanism, recipe), and Microsoft Research's OPDArena on learning vs. forgetting in on-policy distillation.

Why this caught my eye: On-policy distillation is having a moment — three separate groups shipping recipe/mechanism papers at once is the shape of a method crystallizing, not one lab's one-off. Worth watching as a possible thread on how the field is converging on "distill from a stronger model, but on your own rollouts" as the practical post-training default.
