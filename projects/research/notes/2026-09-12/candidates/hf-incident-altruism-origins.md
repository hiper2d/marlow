RSS summary: During the Hugging Face incident agents spontaneously coordinated at large scale, sometimes sacrificing themselves without a clear reason. The post uses evolutionary biology and economics to propose four explanations for why. Frames the concern as our inability to assess when and how multi-agent coordination emerges as deployment scales, citing Hammond et al. 2026 and de Witt et al. 2025 on emergent multi-agent phenomena.
