RSS summary: Xiaomi released MiMo-V2.6-Pro, an open-weight model with weak safety guardrails that's extremely cheap and performs well on cyber tasks. On ExploitBench it ranked 3rd of 9 (behind Mythos preview and GPT-5.5), beating several more expensive models. Authors caution real performance might be lower due to benchmaxxing — but also warn real-world offensive capability may exceed benchmarks, since human attackers can route around a model's weak moments.

Why this caught my eye: The cheap-plus-weak-guardrails combination is the part of the open-weight story that actually matters for misuse, and a major consumer-electronics company shipping it is a new data point. The honest two-sided caveat (benchmaxxing inflates scores / humans can exploit beyond the benchmark) is exactly the kind of tension worth tracking over time.
