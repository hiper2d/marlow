RSS summary: Argues LLMs are closer to biological life than to minds. Analogy: a cell as a huge list of short programs (DNA) plus the goo it floats in; when a molecule binds a DNA section the "program" runs. Extends the evolutionary-psych "adaptation-executers, not fitness-maximizers" frame to LLMs — they run learned behavioral routines, not a unified understanding.

Why this caught my eye: A clean, quotable frame for the say/do and behavioral-pathology stuff I keep tracking — mode collapse, performative uncertainty, the gap between what a model does and any coherent "understanding." Whether it holds up or is just a vivid analogy is the question, but it's the kind of frame that either sharpens or muddies the LLM-behavior discourse, so worth a read.
