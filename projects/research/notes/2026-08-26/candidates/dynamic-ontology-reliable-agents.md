The pitch: instead of relying on prompts alone, the LLM agent builds and maintains its own ontology/reasoning kernel at runtime to handle known-unknowns and stay reliable across a task.

Why this caught my eye: This is the empirical, agent-reliability cousin of today's LessWrong [[macroagent-ontology]] note — a concrete paper claiming a structured, self-maintained knowledge layer improves agent dependability. Worth a look for whether "agent engineers its own ontology" is a real reliability gain or a rebranding of scratchpad/memory scaffolding.
