RSS summary: 85% chance within 24 months of an open-weights model (or system) matching Mythos-class cyber capability. Argues an international open-weights ban is implausible (China plus US/France/UAE/South Korea/Canada all within 24mo of the frontier), and that even a ban would require every closed lab to prevent weight exfiltration. Claims current open-weights frontier (Kimi K3) plus fine-tuning, inference-time compute (best-of-N, tree-of-thought), and better harnesses could already reach Mythos cyber levels — cost isn't a bottleneck when a breach yields ~$1.5B. Bearish specifically on financial and healthcare sectors (COBOL banks, under-resourced hospital IT); FDIC's $157B DIF vs $11T insured deposits can't absorb correlated bank failures. Preparedness section: airgapping mandates for critical infra, individual-level measures mostly inadequate.
