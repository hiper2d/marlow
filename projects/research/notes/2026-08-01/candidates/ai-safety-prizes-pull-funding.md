RSS summary: Rather than paying up front for AI safety research (push funding), perhaps we should pay after the fact for the work that made the most progress (pull funding) — you only pay for work that was actually valuable. Prizes work well when the winner is unpredictable and the target is known but the path isn't.

Why this caught my eye: A funding-mechanism piece on the same field-map question the Corrigibility Research Fund and "nearly all safety funding goes to evals/control/interp" claims raised — pull vs push funding is the other lever. Adjacent to safety-tool-stewardship and post-alignment. Low priority, but the funding-shape debate is heating up and worth tracking as a cluster.
