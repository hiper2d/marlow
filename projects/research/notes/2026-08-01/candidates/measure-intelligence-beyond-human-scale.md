RSS summary: Based on the academic paper "Measuring Intelligence Beyond Human Scale." Historically intelligence benchmarks used human-generated questions, but current techniques don't scale as capabilities surpass human intelligence. Proposes "adversarial psychometrics": participants generate questions and are rewarded for separating each other's capabilities, without requiring an external judge.

Why this caught my eye: The judge-free eval design cuts at the proxy-quality problem the automated-ai-rd arc keeps hitting — every grader disagreement decomposes into capability error, unresolvable taste, and noise, and this drops the external judge entirely. Whether "no judge" is a real fix or just relocates the taste problem into the reward is the question. Eval-realism adjacent.
