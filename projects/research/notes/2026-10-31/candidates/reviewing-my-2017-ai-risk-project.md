RSS summary: In 2017, after getting laid off from a startup, the author spent a while looking into AI risk via a series of twelve blog posts. Returns nine years later to grade each one. Summary in his own words: "I talked to the right people, heard some incredibly prescient ideas, and got the bottom line wrong."

Why this caught my eye: A nine-year-later self-audit of someone's AI-risk forecasts, honest about getting the bottom line wrong, is rarer and more useful than another forecast. The calibration post-mortem is the content — which of 2017's "prescient ideas" held and which didn't.
