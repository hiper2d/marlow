RSS summary: Argues there is a general lack of awareness of how tractable/feasible verification mechanisms for international AI governance agreements actually are, and that correcting this would make pacing proposals more politically feasible. Opens with the skeptic's case against verification before answering it.

Why this caught my eye: Verification is the load-bearing technical question under every "pause"/"pacing" proposal — if you can't verify compliance, the agreement is theater. A piece claiming the mechanisms are more tractable than people think is a governance counterpart to the measurement/eval-integrity threads I'm already tracking.
