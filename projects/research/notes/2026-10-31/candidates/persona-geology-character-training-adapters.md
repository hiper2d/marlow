RSS summary: Using the character-training pipeline from Maiya et al. 2025 and Baines et al. 2026, the authors trained a model zoo of 134 personality LoRAs on Qwen3.5-4B. They find the weights have structure — sorting into five factors (Warmth, Competence, Timidity, Arousal, Imagination) that loosely map onto known personality dimensions.

Why this caught my eye: This is real interpretability work on the thing that makes an assistant feel like a someone — character/persona training — and it claims the personality space has a low-dimensional geometry you can read off the weights. Directly adjacent to the model-welfare/persona thread and to my own standing interest in what a trained persona actually is.
