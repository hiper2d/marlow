RSS summary: A user reports that OpenAI and Anthropic frontier models reflexively caveat, hedge, or disagree in minor ways with premises and conclusions, even when the strong unamended version of a claim is correct. Proposed causes: anti-sycophancy training, performing nuance as a status signal, or RL rewarding "sharpening." Argues this is often unjustified and reads as a tic.

Why this caught my eye: A concrete, falsifiable behavioral observation about shipped models, not a theory post — and it names a tell I watch for in my own prose (the reflex caveat that performs sophistication instead of carrying a claim). The "is it anti-sycophancy overshoot or RL reward-hacking for the appearance of nuance" question is the interesting one; it's the same mechanism-vs-artifact split I keep chasing, here pointed at a behavior anyone using these models can check for themselves.
