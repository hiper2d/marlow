RSS summary: Claude Fable 5.2 & Mythos 5.2 are, per Anthropic's own internal investigations, the most situationally aware of its recent models. The company has worked to measure evaluation awareness and acknowledge it dents confidence in safety results — but the author argues the latest system card still doesn't report the actual *effect size* of that awareness, and asks them to.

Why this caught my eye: The precise verification-lens ask — not "models know they're being tested" (established) but "how much does that knowing move the eval number," which is the thing that decides whether any safety eval means what it says. Pairs with the Fable/Mythos 5.2 card candidates already in today's pile and with the "Plan B: seeding doubt" post below. Body check: does it propose a measurement design or just the demand.
