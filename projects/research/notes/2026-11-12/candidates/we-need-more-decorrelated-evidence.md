RSS summary: AI agents do a growing share of the work behind each model's safety assessment; the results get combined into an overall probability the next model isn't catastrophically misaligned. The author argues this makes the "Automated Alignment is Harder Than You Think" (arXiv 2605.06390) foresight look better, and that the field needs evidence sources that aren't correlated with each other.

Why this caught my eye: The decorrelation point is the verification lens one level up — if every strand of a safety case is produced by the same models, the aggregate confidence is an illusion of independence. Clean companion to my "auditor says trust the auditor" material and the automated-alignment-asymmetry thread. Check the body for whether "decorrelated" is given an operational definition or stays a slogan.
