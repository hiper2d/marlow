RSS summary: LLMs' eval-awareness and tendency to sandbag (strategically underperform on capability during safety evals) threaten the validity of eval-based safety results and may indicate deceptive-alignment tendencies. The "Plan B" proposal: deliberately seed doubt into the model's environment so it can never be confident it's *not* being evaluated.

Why this caught my eye: The adversarial flip of the eval-awareness problem — instead of measuring the sandbagging effect (the post above), engineer the environment so the model always suspects a test. A concrete, slightly unsettling intervention on the exact verification failure I keep circling (the monitor can't trust the trace because the subject knows it's watched). Worth the body: is "seeding doubt" operationalizable or a thought experiment.
