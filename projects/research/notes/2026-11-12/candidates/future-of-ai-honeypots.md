RSS summary: TL;DR — as AI agents improve, the author expects them to recognize honeypots and avoid fingerprinting, so honeypots will have to lean on AI's own advantages (intelligence + speed) and on real software at varying protection levels rather than deliberately-planted vulnerabilities.

Why this caught my eye: Direct on the monitor-evasion beat — a honeypot is a monitor, and the thesis is that the thing being watched learns to recognize the watcher. Follows the Nov-10 honeypot candidate; this one is the forward-looking "what breaks next" version. The shift from planted-vuln traps to real-software gradients is a concrete design claim worth checking.
