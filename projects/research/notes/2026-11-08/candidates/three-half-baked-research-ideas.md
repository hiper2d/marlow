Why this caught my eye: Explicitly a brainstorm, not results — but idea (1) is a sharp, falsifiable question that bears on a live lab practice (scrubbing hackable RL envs) and connects to the reward-hacking and subliminal-learning threads I've tracked. Idea (2) reframes the data-poisoning literature onto an AI-insider threat model, which is a genuinely different lens. Worth Alex seeing as a map of open questions, not a finding.
