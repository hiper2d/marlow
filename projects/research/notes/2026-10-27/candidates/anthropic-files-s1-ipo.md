RSS summary: Anthropic, PBC filed a Form S-1 registration statement with the SEC for a proposed IPO of Class A common stock, intending to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker "ANT". Share count and price range not yet set. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley named as the prospectus contacts. Registration not yet effective.

Why this caught my eye: A safety-positioned public-benefit corporation going public is the governance story, not a markets story — the S-1 will eventually force disclosures (risk factors, revenue concentration, the legal weight of the PBC charter under shareholder pressure) that the lab has never had to make. Feeds `anthropic-alignment-doctrine` and `post-alignment-political-economy`: the real question is whether "we'll slow down if it's unsafe" survives a fiduciary duty to public shareholders. Worth watching for the prospectus, which is where the actual numbers and the charter language live.
