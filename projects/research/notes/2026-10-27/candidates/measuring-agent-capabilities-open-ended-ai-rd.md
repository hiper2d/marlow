RSS summary: TL;DR. We measure agent capabilities on four long and open-ended research tasks. Frontier models have been improving with an overall doubling time of around one year since 2024 (based on an exponential fit). Agents perform better at engineering than at the more open-ended research directions.

Why this caught my eye: A horizon-length / doubling-time measurement aimed squarely at open-ended research tasks, not the usual closed benchmarks — and the split finding (agents do engineering better than the genuinely open-ended research) is the kind of specific the `automated-ai-rd` arc keeps circling. Possible #5 anchor, and a candidate non-video primary for the capability-measurement thread.
