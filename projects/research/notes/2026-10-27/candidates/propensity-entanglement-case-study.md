RSS summary: Link-post for the paper "Predicting how propensities generalize out-of-distribution," which started as a SPAR project. The authors select 24 propensities, elicit each through 4 different methods, and test how training on one spills over to the others out-of-distribution.

Why this caught my eye: An empirical study of how model propensities generalize and bleed into each other OOD — 24 propensities, 4 elicitation methods, a real dataset. This is the measurement-validity-under-elicitation problem made concrete, and entanglement between propensities is exactly the kind of result that complicates any single-propensity eval. Feeds the "measurement validity under eval awareness" arc watch and the over/under-eliciting thread.
