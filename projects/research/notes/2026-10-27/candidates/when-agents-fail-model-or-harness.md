RSS summary: Walkthrough of a Shanghai AI Lab paper, "When Agents Fail: Model or Harness—Who's to Blame?" Attributes agent failures to the model vs. the scaffolding/harness, with a focus on open-weight models (Kimi, Qwen, DeepSeek) across harnesses (OpenCode, Codex, Claude Code).

Why this caught my eye: The model-vs-harness attribution question is exactly the thing most agent benchmarks blur — a weak score gets read as "the model can't," when half the time it's the scaffolding dropping tool calls or mangling context. A paper that tries to decompose that, specifically on open-weight models across real harnesses, is worth the primary read over the video summary.
