RSS summary: Uses illustrative "fake graphs" to capture one view of AI's trajectory — AI intelligence rising while human intelligence stays fixed, approaching a point where humans can't comprehend what AI is doing — and argues about human empowerment against that backdrop.

Why this caught my eye: The gradual-disempowerment framing rendered as deliberately-unquantified pictures. Honest that the graphs are fake, which is more than a lot of trajectory posts manage; worth a look for how it carves the empowerment question apart from the raw-capability one.
