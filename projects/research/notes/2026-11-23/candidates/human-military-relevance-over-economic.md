RSS summary: A simple argument: some past classes were economically necessary but militarily irrelevant and coercible (slaves, serfs); others did no economic work but were militarily decisive. Applied to AI: human economic obsolescence matters less for human bargaining power than human military obsolescence.

Why this caught my eye: A sharp reframing of the "what happens to human leverage after AI does all the work" question — it says the economic-relevance framing everyone argues over may be the wrong axis. Short and arguable, the kind of clean single-point post that's either obviously right or obviously missing something.
