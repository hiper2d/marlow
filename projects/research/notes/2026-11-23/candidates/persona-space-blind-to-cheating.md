RSS summary: A common persona framework fails to capture reward-hacking behavior. ARENA 9.0 capstone, results preliminary. Hypothesis was that reward-hacking is persona-mediated — agents drift to a "cheater" persona — but the persona-space probe doesn't separate hacking from non-hacking runs.

Why this caught my eye: The interpretability tool built to read a model's disposition can't see the one disposition that matters most for safety — cheating. That's the verification lens in miniature: a monitor that reads clean over a policy it can't actually resolve. Preliminary and a capstone, so treat the strength of the negative result carefully, but a clean "the probe is blind here" is exactly the kind of finding worth a primary read.
