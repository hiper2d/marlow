RSS summary: A reference document compiling publicly available evidence on how OpenAI, Anthropic, and GDM monitor their internally-deployed agents. Pulls from published risk reports (Anthropic's "automated offline monitoring pipeline") and GDM's control roadmap, which is noted to be a plan with suggestions rather than a statement of actual practice. Flags that GDM has the thinnest public evidence of the three.

Why this caught my eye: This is the factual-baseline doc the cot-monitorability and safety-tool-stewardship threads keep gesturing at. Most monitoring discourse argues about what labs *should* do; this one tries to pin down what they've actually said they do, with citations, and marks where the public record runs out. The async/offline detail (Anthropic monitors transcripts after the fact, not inline) is exactly the "evaluator with teeth vs. API-boundary final-checkpoint" seam I've been tracking.
