RSS summary: Anthropic merges Project Glasswing and the Cyber Verification Program into one tiered offering (Defense / Red Team / Specialized Access), each with different verification requirements and classifier safeguards, exposing advanced cyber capabilities to vetted security teams. Includes CyScenarioBench efficacy numbers per tier and Glasswing vulnerability-finding totals.

Why this caught my eye: This is the operational counterpart to the cyber-eval-framing thread — not another benchmark claim, but a lab deciding who gets to turn the safeguards *off* and building an access-tier structure around dual-use risk. The CyScenarioBench numbers are the interesting part: Opus 5.5 is blocked on every task with no access, blocked on 46/50 at Defense tier, and hits its unsafeguarded 67.6% success rate at Red Team tier — a rare published look at a safeguard classifier's actual block rate, not just a capability score. The 129,000 "verified vulnerabilities" from Glasswing (Apr–Jul) is the headline number worth interrogating: survey-based, subset of partners, self-reported time savings. Feeds cyber-eval-framing directly — the arc has been about measuring offensive capability; this is about governing access to it.
