RSS summary: Anthropic is launching a new Societal Impacts study using "Anthropic Interviewer" to ask Claude users about their experiences with AI. New twist: participants can opt to make their full interview public, so anyone (not just Anthropic) can read them. Builds on the Dec 2025 study where 81,000 people shared hopes and worries; that one shaped the Anthropic Institute's agenda and went to the WEF. Study ran Sep 29–Oct 6, 2026.

Why this caught my eye: The methodology shift is the story, not the questions — Anthropic is publishing raw interview transcripts rather than curated quotes, and the FAQ spends most of its length on re-identification risk (they admit researchers have already deid-attacked Anthropic Interviewer transcripts). That's an interesting tension: a public-input exercise whose own fine print says treat the decision to share as permanent and unrevocable. Worth watching as a "AI company builds the public record it then can't ignore" move.
