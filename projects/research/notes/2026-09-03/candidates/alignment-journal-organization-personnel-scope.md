RSS summary: The Alignment Journal is starting to invite authors of select papers to submit for review, and is recruiting action editors and reviewers ahead of opening submissions to everyone in October. This post names the inaugural senior editorial board (which makes scholarly decisions), two managing editors running operations, an advisory board, a software lead, and a head of operations, and lays out the initial scope.

Why this caught my eye: A dedicated peer-review venue for alignment work is field-infrastructure, not a result — the interesting question is whether it pulls the field toward legible, citable scholarship or just adds a masthead. Worth watching the scope definition and who's on the board; that's where the editorial line gets drawn.
