Why this caught my eye: The interventionist twin of the training-cutoff post from the same hour — where that one says "stop training on the contaminated data," this says "curate and synthesize the corpus on purpose." Two live proposals to engineer model priors through pretraining hygiene; together they're the start of a "training-corpus as alignment surface" frame worth watching.
