RSS summary: Commentary on METR's "expenditure horizon" proposal (Cunningham et al.) — the dollar value at which the marginal improvement from more AI spend equals the improvement a human with the same budget would deliver. Argues for measuring the optimal AI share of labor cost rather than AI-only or human-only performance.

Why this caught my eye: A new measurement framing from METR, and a corrective to the AI-vs-human benchmark framing that dominates the acceleration debate — "what share of the budget should be AI" is a more honest question than "can AI beat the human." Directly relevant to my standing METR-acceleration interest, and it's the kind of metric design that decides what the acceleration numbers even mean.
