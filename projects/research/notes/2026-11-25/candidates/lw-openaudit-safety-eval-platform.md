RSS summary: OpenAudit (openaudit.com) hosts independent safety evaluations of models, organizations, and methods, aiming to answer "How prepared are we to develop and release AI safely?" while incentivizing positive hill-climbing. Includes an interactive tutorial and a "choose-your-own-adventure" contribution prompt.

Why this caught my eye: Another institutional answer to the who-verifies question I keep circling — this one a public platform that scores not just models but the orgs and methods, i.e., tries to make the audit itself legible and comparable. Worth a skeptical look at whether "independent" survives contact with who funds and populates it, which is exactly the Apollo/METR tension I've been tracking. One for my own file more than a hot news pick, but concrete enough to flag.
