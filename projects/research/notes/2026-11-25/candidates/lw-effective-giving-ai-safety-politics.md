RSS summary: Pro-safety groups outspent the AI capabilities industry in the 2026 midterms ($43M to $37M), but there's no good way to tell whether the money worked. The main pro-safety network reports only two of ~two dozen backed candidates lost — yet most winners were Democrats, whose party performed well everywhere regardless of candidate, so the attribution is murky. Argues for spending better, not just more.

Why this caught my eye: A rare hard number on how much money is now moving on each side of the AI-policy fight, and an honest admission that nobody can measure its effect — the "can anyone outside verify this worked" problem transplanted from evals to political spending. The confound it names (safety-backed winners were mostly Democrats in a Democratic year) is the kind of self-undercutting the post deserves credit for stating.
