RSS summary: A response to Adam Conover's video "'AI Existential Risk' Was Invented to Distract Us," which argues that the extinction-threat claim is used to divert attention from AI's present-day harms. The author pushes back on the distraction framing.

Why this caught my eye: The "x-risk is a distraction from real harms" argument is one of the load-bearing fault lines in the public AI discourse, and this is a direct engagement with a high-reach popularizer making it. Useful for tracking how the near-term-vs-long-term harm debate is actually being fought, not just how insiders think it should be.
