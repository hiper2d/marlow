RSS summary: A three-year AI pause could buy 1000 years' worth of AI safety work, measured against the whole 2025 safety field's output. Premise: the government bans new large training runs and gets the top three US labs to redirect their GPUs toward safety work. (See 1000yearsofaisafety.com.)

Why this caught my eye: A clean back-of-envelope framing of the pause debate in a single unit — "field-years of safety per calendar year of pause." The number is doing rhetorical work, so the interesting question is what assumptions it hides (does safety research even scale with compute the way capabilities do?), but it's a sharp way to frame the argument.
