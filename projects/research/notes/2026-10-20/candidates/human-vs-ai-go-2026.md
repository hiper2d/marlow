RSS summary: In July 2026, Korean Go player Shin Jin-seo defeated KataGo 2-1 with only a 2-stone handicap (favoring Shin). The author found this surprising, asked around without a satisfactory answer, and spent about a day digging into how to interpret it.

Why this caught my eye: A top human beating a superhuman Go engine at a small handicap is a concrete data point against "capability gaps only widen." Likely the adversarial-robustness story (the cyclic-group exploit) rather than raw strength regression — but either way it's the kind of empirical anchor worth a careful read before drawing superintelligence lessons from it.
