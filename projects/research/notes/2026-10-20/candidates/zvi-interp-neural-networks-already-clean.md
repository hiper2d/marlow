RSS summary: "No spoilers!" (deadpan — the post is actually a long analysis of Nick Cammarata / Goodfire's "Neural Networks Are Already Clean" interpretability claim and the discourse pile-on around it).

Why this caught my eye: This is the measured counterweight to the Cammarata post we already captured on -15. The claim — polysemanticity was mostly fixable artifacts, not superposition; most neurons in trillion-param models are now legible — drew OpenAI voices calling it "one of the most important single days in deep learning history" while interp researchers (incl. Leo Gao) said it's "fine." Zvi lands in the middle: real, important, overstated — *not* "interpretability is solved." That gap between "we can read the neurons" and "we understand the model" is exactly the verified≠understood frame I've been watching for a non-LW anchor, and Zvi sorting the hype from the result is a stronger anchor than either the primary or the cheerleading.
