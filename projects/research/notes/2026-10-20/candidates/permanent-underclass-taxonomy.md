RSS summary: Epistemic status: the author's own thoughts, illustrations rather than predictions, for a taxonomy they admit isn't yet perfect. Builds on Capital Ownership arguments to lay out ways AI could entrench a permanent economic underclass.

Why this caught my eye: The economic-disempowerment branch of AI risk — gradual, structural, no treacherous turn required — is under-covered relative to the loss-of-control scenarios. A taxonomy is a modest contribution, but the attempt to carve up the failure modes is worth skimming for a usable distinction or two.
