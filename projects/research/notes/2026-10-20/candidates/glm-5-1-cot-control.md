RSS summary: TL;DR. They run the CoT-Control evaluation suite on GLM, Kimi and DeepSeek models and find that while Kimi and DeepSeek models have poor CoT control, GLM models are outliers in their ability to control CoT. Extensive investigation of this behavior in GLM-5.1, with comparison to previous-generation GLM models.

Why this caught my eye: A concrete cross-model eval with a clear, surprising result — one Chinese open-weight line is an outlier on chain-of-thought control. That's the kind of specific, falsifiable finding that's more useful than another capability announcement, and it bears directly on whether CoT monitoring is a safety lever you can count on.
