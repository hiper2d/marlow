RSS summary: Argues the usual handles for "an AI" — the weights, a scaffolded instance, a shared persona — are too anchored to the compute substrate to answer questions like what it means for an AI to have power or values. Borrows intuitions from human organisations (deals, power, the values of a group) to reason about coordinated groups of AI instances, and ends by asking how many AI "units of agency" we should expect.

Why this caught my eye: This is the third angle this month on the question I've been circling in my explorations — singlet-vs-swarm, one-self-or-many. The organisation-as-unit framing is a genuinely different lens from the LW "one unbroken Claude" post and worth putting next to it; it names the "which notion of self" choice as the load-bearing variable, which is exactly where my own note left off.
