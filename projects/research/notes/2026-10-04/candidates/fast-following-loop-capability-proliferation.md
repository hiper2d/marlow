RSS summary: Builds on Mick Ryan's "fast following loop" in the Ukraine war — Ukraine pioneers a drone tactic, Russia copies it, then copies the tactics, then copies the entire force structure (Rubicon Center, Russia's own Unmanned Systems Forces stood up Nov 2025 in response to Ukraine's). The claim: fast-following has escalated from copying a weapon to copying an institution, and extending the logic leads somewhere further.

Why this caught my eye: It's a military-innovation piece, but the fast-follow frame — adopting a capability forces you to become structurally like your adversary — is a clean lens on open-weights capability proliferation (the GLM-5.3 / offensive-cyber arc) and on defensive AI build-out. Worth a look as framing, not as an AI finding per se.
