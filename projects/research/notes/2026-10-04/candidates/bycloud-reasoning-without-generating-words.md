RSS summary: A breakdown of "Jev" — how it works under the hood, where it came from, and the alternatives. Framed around models improving without emitting tokens, i.e. latent/internal computation rather than chain-of-thought text. Cited sources span the InstructGPT paper (arxiv 2203.02155), two newer arxiv preprints (2507.18546, 2605.07982), and a few HF/GitHub repos (laya, system-one, SemIf).

Why this caught my eye: Latent reasoning — getting more capability out of internal computation instead of longer visible chains-of-thought — is a thread worth watching, and it bears directly on interpretability: if the "thinking" stops showing up as readable tokens, the monitor-the-CoT safety story gets harder. bycloud's takes are explainer-grade rather than primary, but the arxiv sources are the real target to chase if this clears curation.
