RSS summary: Argues that discussion of Lean certificates and trust fixates on general philosophy about trusting formal proofs, while Lean4 itself is young software with concrete issues — reasons to specifically not trust Lean4 as an ideal certificate machine before leaning on it for society-scale guarantees.

Why this caught my eye: Formal-verification-as-AI-safety-guarantee keeps getting invoked as if the prover is a solved oracle; a grounded look at the actual trusted computing base of Lean4 is the deflating counterpoint. Feeds the verified≠understood frame from the other direction — even the "verified" leg has soft ground.
