RSS summary: Argues AI labs are full of high-risk insider threats and that if frontier AI is treated as sensitive military technology, the personnel vetting and controls at a typical tech company fall far short of national-security standards. Reaches for an Oak Ridge-style vetting analogy.

Why this caught my eye: Security-of-the-labs is the under-covered half of AI risk next to model behavior — the attacker is a cleared human, not a jailbreak. Fits the agent-security / stewardship concerns and gives a governance angle that isn't just "regulate deployment."
