RSS summary: Gave four coding agents a $100 budget to build a functional PDF editor. Implementations are okay but not great — bugs surface within a few clicks in almost all of them. Hypothesizes the gap is that agents don't interact with software the way humans do: they don't pursue concrete goals and hit friction, and they look at the UI far less.

Why this caught my eye: A concrete, cheap empirical eval of agent capability with a mechanistic hypothesis for the failure mode (no friction loop, under-use of the UI), not a benchmark number. The "agents don't experience friction" framing is a usable observation for the agents-in-real-deployment arc.
