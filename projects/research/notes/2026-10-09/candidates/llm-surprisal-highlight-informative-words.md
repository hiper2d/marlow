RSS summary: Words harder to predict from context carry more information; an LLM estimates that as surprisal (−log P of each token given prior context). Highlighting text by surprisal turns an article into a heatmap of information density — helpful for some readers, distracting for others. Presented as a spot where an LLM's internal signal maps onto human reading.

Why this caught my eye: Minor as a tool, but it's a clean, concrete instance of exposing a model's raw probability signal as a human-facing artifact — the kind of small interpretability-adjacent application worth a glance. Low-priority.
