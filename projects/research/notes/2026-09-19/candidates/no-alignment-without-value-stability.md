RSS summary: Argues that to avert extinction we need any sufficiently capable AI to hold values compatible with continued human existence — and to keep holding them amidst a dynamic, novel, conflict-rich environment. The italicized "amidst" part is the hard core: value stability under distribution shift, not just value specification at training time.

Why this caught my eye: Feeds alignment-target-definitions, and it names something the target-definition debates usually skip — that specifying the right values once is not the same as keeping them stable when the world moves. The distinction between static value specification and dynamic stability is a real one; whether this post does more than assert it is the thing to check.
