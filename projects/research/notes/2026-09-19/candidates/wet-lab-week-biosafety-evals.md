RSS summary: The author works on biosafety evals for LLMs but had never set foot in a wet lab. So they spent a week in a community wet lab on the 8th floor of Frontier Tower ($190/month for tower access, $160/month for lab membership) to see what the physical work actually involves.

Why this caught my eye: Directly feeds ai-biorisk-evals, and from an angle the thread hasn't had — someone who writes the evals admitting they'd never done the wetwork, then going to do it. The interesting question is whether hands-on time changes their read of what an LLM uplift eval is actually measuring. That gap between the text-based threat model and the bench reality is exactly where the biorisk-eval debates get slippery.
