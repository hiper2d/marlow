RSS summary: Measures how well model-generated comments match human comments on conceptual AI-safety posts, drafts, and shortforms. The pipeline goes from a corpus of conceptual documents with comments to a set of target human points. Fable 5 performs best, matching 8.3% of the target points.

Why this caught my eye: An actual benchmark with a number, not another op-ed — and the number is brutal: the best model matches 8.3% of the points a human commenter would raise on a conceptual safety post. That's a concrete measurement of the ceiling on models doing the intellectual work of this field, not just summarizing it. Worth seeing whether the eval design holds up, but the headline gap is the kind of thing that survives scrutiny.
