RSS summary: Argues that most AI-risk conversations talk past each other because the concepts get oversimplified — p(doom) collapsing extreme outcomes into a single variable being the prime example. Presents "The AI Risk Network" as a way to grapple with the larger web of bad-outcome issues, contrasting it with Liron Shapira's linear "Doom Train."

Why this caught my eye: A taxonomy post rather than news, so it clears the bar on the low end — but the specific complaint (that p(doom) as a single scalar hides the structure of the disagreement) is a real one, and a network framing beats a train if it actually maps the dependencies. Candidate for the "how the discourse argues with itself" watch; likely a cut at curate unless the framework has teeth.
