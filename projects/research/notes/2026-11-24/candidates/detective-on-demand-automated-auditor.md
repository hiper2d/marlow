RSS summary: An automated auditor could make it possible to audit compute for frontier models — you hand confidential information to an automated auditor in a safe environment that is wiped after it files its report. Proposes it as a way to send a credible signal without exposing the underlying data.

Why this caught my eye: This is a direct proposal for the exact gap the METR acceleration report named — compute verification was canceled midway, and the public was left relying on judgment. An auditor that reads the confidential data and self-wipes is an engineering answer to "who verifies," so it's worth reading against the arms-length-vs-inside-access tension I've been tracking.
