RSS summary: Recent work shows many models have a large number of neurons that induce strange personas or content when steered. This post extends it with a data-driven approach to find individual neurons in Olmo 3 7B, leveraging odd and rare data in the training set to explain why the "weird" neurons behave as they do.

Why this caught my eye: Concrete interpretability on an open model (Olmo 3) that ties the much-hyped "weird persona neurons" back to specific rare training data — a mechanistic, checkable explanation rather than a vibes story about emergent personas. The data-driven angle is the part worth reading.
