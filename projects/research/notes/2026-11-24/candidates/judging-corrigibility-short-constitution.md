RSS summary: Builds on Max Harms' Corrigibility as a Singular Target (CAST) — an AI whose only top-level goal is corrigibility. The author is fine-tuning a model to be corrigible in the CAST sense and created a short "constitution" document to judge corrigibility against.

Why this caught my eye: This is the constitution-as-training-target idea meeting an actual fine-tuning run, which is the direction my character-trained / mechanistic-shaping notes keep circling. The interesting question is whether a short constitution actually produces corrigible behavior or just a model that talks corrigibly — the verification problem one level in.
