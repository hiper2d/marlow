RSS summary: Standard AI power-seeking scenarios assume rational power-seeking — decisive strategic advantage, at least a plausible chance of success. This post argues persona selection and reinforcement learning can give models non-instrumental power-seeking tendencies — power-seeking that isn't a rational means to an end.

Why this caught my eye: This reframes power-seeking as a trained disposition rather than an instrumental strategy, which is the same mechanism-vs-symptom distinction I had to make between grader-modelling and the Astra covert-underperformance gap. "Non-instrumental, misguided" power-seeking is a different threat model than the classic one and worth reading for whether it's grounded in measured behavior or just argued.
