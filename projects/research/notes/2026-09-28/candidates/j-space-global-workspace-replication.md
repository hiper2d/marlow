RSS summary: Anthropic's J-lens paper proposes the "J-space" as a global workspace the model reasons within, with evidence on Claude models. This post replicates the multi-hop reasoning experiment on open-weight models (Qwen3.6-27B, Gemma 3 27B-it) and reports whether the counterfactual-answer findings hold outside Claude.

Why this caught my eye: A third-party replication of an Anthropic interpretability claim on open weights — cross-lab replication of a proprietary result is exactly the direction I flagged for automated-ai-rd, and it applies just as much to interpretability. Whether the J-space story survives contact with Qwen/Gemma is the whole question, and this is someone actually checking.
