RSS summary: Write-up from Generality Labs' Inspect Evals Data Viz Hackathon — using Inspect AI and Scout to plot failure modes on Humanity's Last Exam (HLE) tasks, digging into what's actually driving the errors rather than the headline pass rate.

Why this caught my eye: Eval-quality content, not eval-scoreboard content — the question of *why* a model fails an HLE item (bad reasoning vs. bad grading vs. ambiguous question) is the same verifier-reliability seam the 5-LLM disagreement and "verified≠understood" candidates keep hitting. Built on Inspect AI, which is worth tracking as eval infrastructure.
