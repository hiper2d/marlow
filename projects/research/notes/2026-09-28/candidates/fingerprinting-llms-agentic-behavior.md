RSS summary: "Who Is Behind the Harness? Fingerprinting LLMs through Agentic Behavior" — Chuyi Wang, Xiaohui Xie, Tongze Wang, Fangchen Luo, Yong Cui, Tsinghua University Dept of Computer Science and Technology. Claims ~958 behavioral features that identify which model is driving an agent.

Why this caught my eye: Fingerprinting a model from how it *acts* in a harness — not from weights or a watermark — is the flip side of the monitorability arc. If behavioral signatures are that stable, they're both an audit tool (you can tell which model actually ran) and an attack surface (an adversary can too). Wants the primary paper checked before it goes anywhere. Possible crossover with `cot-monitorability` and the agent-security frame.
