RSS summary: Linkpost to a new essay by futurist/SF author Ramez Naam. AI is already helping improve itself; the question is whether fully autonomous recursive self-improvement (RSI) would produce a runaway intelligence explosion, or whether diminishing returns dominate at each generation.

Why this caught my eye: Feeds the automated-ai-rd arc from the skeptical side — the recent nine-loop and Riemann-zeta results landed on "recombination + compute, not new insight," which is exactly the diminishing-returns hypothesis stated empirically. Naam arguing it from the economics of self-improvement pairs well, and it lands the same day as the Yudkowsky FOOM dialogue for a natural contrast.
