RSS summary: Epistemic status >90% on the principle. Argues per-run FLOP caps are hard to enforce because runs can be chained or parallel runs aggregated, and weight smuggling defeats verification mechanisms that try to prevent this. No full implementation yet.

Why this caught my eye: Compute-governance enforceability is the mechanism sitting under every "pace the frontier" governance proposal — post-alignment-political-economy tracks the policy, this tracks whether the policy is even physically enforceable. A specific "here's the loophole" argument beats another op-ed about whether caps are a good idea.
