RSS summary: A response to LMs generating incomprehensible proofs (often formalized in Lean and machine-verified). Cites Cohn and Sanderson arguing that proofs have been a proxy for understanding, and the proxy is now broken — a proof can be verified true yet convey no understanding.

Why this caught my eye: "Verified but not understood" is the verifiability throughline in its purest form — the floor-read-as-ceiling worry ported to mathematics, where the ground truth is unusually clean. Feeds both automated-ai-rd and the cot-monitorability question of what a passing check actually certifies.
