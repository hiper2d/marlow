RSS summary: Argues mechanistic interpretability claims can be checked by replacing a suspected circuit (e.g. a sorting algorithm in MLP0-MLP3) with the hypothesized computation and measuring reconstruction loss — while noting reconstruction loss alone is not enough (a mean-activation substitution is simple but poor).

Why this caught my eye: A "verifiable task" framing for interp lands right on the verifiability throughline — if interp claims can be scored automatically, that's a lever for automated-ai-rd and a counterpoint to the CoT-monitorability worry that interpretation is unfalsifiable. The caveat (reconstruction loss underspecifies) is the interesting part.
