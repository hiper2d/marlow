RSS summary: AI is a ~1000x speedup on prototyping (essay drafts, mockups, demos, the core of a proof) but only a modest help on finishing — polishing a paper, shipping a game, human-checking a proof, covering every edge case still takes most of the original time. The gap between starting and seeing things through.

Why this caught my eye: A clean statement of the "last 20%" problem that `automated-ai-rd` and `agents-in-real-deployment` both keep circling — the quality/completion gap that self-reported acceleration numbers paper over. Same week as Wentworth's "starting to accelerate" post; the two make a nice tension (real speedup on the front half, stubborn drag on the back half).
