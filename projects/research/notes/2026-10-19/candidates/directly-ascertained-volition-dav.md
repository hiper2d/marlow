RSS summary: Proposes Directly Ascertained Volition as a development of Yudkowsky's 2004 Coherent Extrapolated Volition. Argues CEV's "idealised-me" can diverge sharply from actual-me (the galaxy-brained argument for abandoning your family), so DAV aims for a more granular, per-individual elicitation of values with an action plan.

Why this caught my eye: Alignment-target theorizing in the CEV lineage — fits the alignment-target-definitions arc. Speculative solo proposal rather than empirical work, so a lower-priority candidate, but it's a clean restatement of the idealised-vs-actual-values tension that keeps that arc alive.
