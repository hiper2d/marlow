RSS summary: Two-week Redwood Research internship sprint. Catalogs the kinds of lies researchers tell models across 292 public alignment evaluations (Dec 2022–Oct 2026), with a figure breaking down the percentage of evals containing each type of lie. Argues these lies are useful both practically and scientifically but have under-examined costs.

Why this caught my eye: A census of deception *by* the evaluators, not the models — directly relevant to eval-framing and whether our safety measurements are contaminated by the fiction we feed models. 292 evals is a real sample, not a vibe. Pairs with the model-welfare and evaluation-validity threads.
