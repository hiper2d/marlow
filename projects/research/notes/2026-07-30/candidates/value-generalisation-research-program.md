RSS summary: A three-part series (all posted 2026-07-29) proposing "value generalisation" — an AI correctly extending human values and preferences to situations neither it nor we have seen — as a distinct research and deployment program. Part 1 is the program pitch (looking for people, advice, funding). Part 2 ("The Missing Hole in AIs' abilities") argues the gap isn't capability but the human superpower of extending values into novel situations. Part 3 ("Pre-aligned AIs") sketches building generalising AIs that don't force the usual alignment-vs-capabilities tradeoff.
