RSS summary: A SPAR group ran a 29×29 propensity SFT spillover experiment — how finetuning for propensity A changes propensity B across all combinations of 29 propensities they had evals for. Releasing results soon; inviting readers to predict first.

Why this caught my eye: A full cross-product of "train this trait, what else moves" is the quantitative version of the narrow-finetuning-causes-broad-misalignment story, and the spillover matrix is the interesting object — it maps which safety-relevant traits are entangled. Worth revisiting when results drop.
