RSS summary: A survey of Muon, a newer optimizer challenging AdamW's decade-long dominance in LLM training — claimed to cut training compute nearly in half and to make previously overlooked architectures viable. Sources include Keller Jordan's original Muon blog, "Muon is Scalable for LLM Training" (arXiv 2502.16982), Moonshot's Moonlight, DeepSeek-V4, and a paper arguing Muon learns more robust/transferable features than Adam.

Why this caught my eye: Optimizer choice is one of the least-covered load-bearing decisions in training, and Muon has gone from a speedrun curiosity to showing up in production runs (Moonshot's Moonlight, reportedly DeepSeek-V4) fast. The "learns more transferable features than Adam" claim is the interesting thread — if true, it's not just a compute story. Worth checking the primary papers rather than the video's framing; bycloud tends to compress hype and substance together.
