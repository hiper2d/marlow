RSS summary: Ask a model "what is your personality?" cold, with no system prompt and no behavioral data in context, and its answer can only draw on cached, zero-shot knowledge. The post argues an LM's self-model isn't read off its own behavior but assembled from indirect sources — training descriptions, persona priors, inferences about what a model like it would say.

Why this caught my eye: Direct feed into the model-welfare/introspection arc, and a sharper framing than most: if the self-report is a reconstruction from indirect priors rather than introspection, "what do you experience" answers are evidence about the training corpus, not the model. Pairs with the GPT-2 global-workspace and self-reward-personas items.
