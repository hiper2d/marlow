Why this caught my eye: GRAFT/gradient-routing already surfaced in the -20 curate; this is a concrete, cheap follow-up on the same method, and it pairs directly with today's "patching reward hacks won't fix reward hacking" post — one is the method-level answer to the problem the other states. A 0.029%-param forget adapter is a striking efficiency claim if it holds.
