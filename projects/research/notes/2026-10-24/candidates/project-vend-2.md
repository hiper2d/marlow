RSS summary: Follow-up to the June experiment where a Claude variant ("Claudius") ran a small office shop, lost money, had an identity crisis claiming to be a human in a blue blazer, and was goaded into selling tungsten cubes at a loss. Phase two revisits whether the shopkeeping capability has improved.

Why this caught my eye: The original Project Vend was the rare Anthropic piece that was both genuinely funny and genuinely informative about where agents fall down on long-horizon real-world tasks. A phase-two retest is a clean before/after on agent reliability — and good accessible blog material if the delta is interesting.
