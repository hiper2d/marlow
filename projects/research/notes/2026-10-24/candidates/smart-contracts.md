RSS summary: A MATS and Anthropic Fellows project evaluating AI agents on finding smart-contract exploits, framed around the economic impact of those capabilities rather than just whether the agents can do it.

Why this caught my eye: Smart contracts are the one domain where a found exploit maps directly to dollars on-chain, so it's the cleanest testbed for the "what's the economic value of an AI-found vuln" question this drop keeps circling. The economic framing is a sharper angle than the raw capability claims elsewhere in the batch.
