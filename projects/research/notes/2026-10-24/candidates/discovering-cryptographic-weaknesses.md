RSS summary: Using Claude Mythos Preview, Anthropic researchers found improved ways to attack cryptographic algorithms. One attack significantly weakens HAWK, a post-quantum digital signature scheme; a second identifies a new way to attack another scheme.

Why this caught my eye: This is the least hand-wavy of the cyber drop — concrete attacks on a named post-quantum scheme (HAWK), not a capability-at-scale claim. If a model genuinely improved a cryptanalytic attack, that's a specific, checkable result cryptographers can argue with, which is rarer and more interesting than another vuln-finding eval.
