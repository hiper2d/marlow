RSS summary: Anthropic is going beyond assessing nuclear-proliferation risk to building tooling, extending its partnership with the US Department of Energy's National Nuclear Security Administration (NNSA) begun last April, to keep models from handing users dangerous weapons-relevant technical knowledge.

Why this caught my eye: The dual-use nuclear case is the sharpest version of the "what should a model refuse" problem, and a private company co-building safeguards with NNSA is a notable governance precedent. Worth watching how much of the actual classifier/tooling they disclose versus gesture at.
