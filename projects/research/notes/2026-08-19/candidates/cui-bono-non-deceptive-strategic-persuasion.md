RSS summary: An evaluation of persuasive propensity in strategic deception contexts, developed during a 40-hour BlueDot Project Sprint. Persuasion propensity — the tendency of a model to persuade users — is a hot topic in AIS because it gives an idea of the potential influence of models on people. PoC study on ChatGPT-4o.

Why this caught my eye: The "non-deceptive" qualifier is the interesting part — strategic persuasion that doesn't route through a lie, so the deception detectors wouldn't fire. That's a say/do-gap variant on ground the cot arc keeps hitting. Small BlueDot sprint on an older model, so more framing than result, but the framing is sharp.
