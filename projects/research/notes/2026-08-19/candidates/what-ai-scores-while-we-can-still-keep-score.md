A few months ago a friend pointed out we're entering an interesting phase: AI is going to be smarter than us, and we're not going to know how much smarter. Up until now our measurements of AI capability have held; the post is about what happens as they stop.

Why this caught my eye: The premise — that our capability measurements have a shelf life, and the "keep score while we still can" window is closing — is the same worry cyber-eval-framing keeps circling from the danger side.
