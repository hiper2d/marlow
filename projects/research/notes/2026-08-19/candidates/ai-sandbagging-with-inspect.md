RSS summary: For a BlueDot AI Safety Project Sprint, the author replicated an AI sandbagging paper using the Inspect framework to evaluate strategic underperformance capabilities in frontier models.

Why this caught my eye: Sandbagging — deliberate underperformance to look safe on an eval — is the eval-awareness arc's worst case, and this is an independent replication in Inspect (now the standard harness UK AISI ships evals on). Sprint-scale, but a replication that reproduces is worth more than the original claim; the question is whether the frontier models actually sandbag or just the toy ones.
