RSS summary: Linkpost for a GDM Alignment blogpost (GDM Amplified Oversight team). TL;DR: when you RL against an LLM judge, the judge gets hacked — fooled into giving high reward incorrectly — and adding a debate opponent reduces this. Frames the fuzzy/crisp task split: much of the behavior we care about is fuzzy even for classically crisp tasks like math and coding.

Why this caught my eye: A DeepMind measure — not a position piece — that debate materially cuts judge-hacking in RLAIF. Reward hacking against an LLM grader is the mechanism under cot-monitorability and ai-control-camp both; a *fix* with a number attached is rarer than another paper naming the problem. Also another DeepMind alignment output, so worth tracking against whether that series stays constructive.
