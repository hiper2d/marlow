RSS summary: First post of a project measuring the current state of zero-knowledge proofs for LLM inference, aimed at compute verification for international agreements. Includes a baseline overhead measurement and the "hash problem." Headed toward a paper.

Why this caught my eye: Compute verification is the plumbing under any enforceable AI treaty, and most governance writing assumes it exists. An actual overhead number for ZKP-over-inference is the kind of concrete datapoint that tells you whether the policy proposals are science fiction or not yet.
