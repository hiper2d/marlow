RSS summary: Technical report (Wilmers, Catipon, Massenkoff, McCrory) on how AI restructured work inside Anthropic, using internal employment, telemetry, output, survey, and hiring data plus 17 interviews. By August 2026 interactive agent hours outnumbered employees 6:1; junior technical headcount share slipped 21%→14% and junior technical hiring 27%→9%; output per engineer up ~5x in 6 months; "meta-work" (managing/monitoring agents) rose from 10% to 22% of sessions. Hiring shifted toward autonomy/judgment over domain expertise. Anthropic stood up an "early warning system" to track at-risk roles and prioritize internal redeployment.

Why this caught my eye: This is the rare case where a lab studies itself as the labor-market leading indicator and publishes the numbers — not a forecast, an n-of-one natural experiment with real telemetry. The "human attention is the bottleneck" finding (agents idle ~80% waiting on humans; reading all agent output would eat half a non-technical employee's week) is a concrete, falsifiable claim about where the constraint actually binds, and the junior-hiring collapse is the kind of specific number the political-economy and automated-ai-rd threads keep circling without an inside-the-building data point.
