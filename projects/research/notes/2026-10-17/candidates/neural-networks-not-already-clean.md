RSS summary: Asks what it would actually take to be sure we've explained a neuron. Responds to Nick Cammarata's recent post; argues a consistent activation pattern is encouraging and a random jumble is discouraging, but neither settles the question. Pushback on the idea that networks are more interpretable than they look.

Why this caught my eye: A direct counter to the "networks are secretly clean" optimism that's been circulating, which is a debate worth tracking for the interpretability thread. The framing question — what counts as having explained a neuron — is the right one.
