RSS summary: LASR Labs Summer 2026 project, supervised by Justin Olive (Generality Labs) and Magda Dubois (UK AISI), on making capability evaluations scale. Framed as groundwork for the evals pipeline that safety cases lean on.

Why this caught my eye: Evals are the load-bearing part of every "we tested it and it's fine" claim, and the scaling bottleneck is real. AISI involvement makes this closer to what regulators will actually use than a one-off benchmark.
