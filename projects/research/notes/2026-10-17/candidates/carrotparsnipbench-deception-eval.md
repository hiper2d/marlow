RSS summary: Builds on the author's earlier Carrot-Parsnip social-deduction game. Most models are better-than-random at identifying the hidden deceptive role (Parsnip), and being a strong deceiver does not perfectly track being a strong detector. Packaged as a lightweight multi-agent deception/detection benchmark.

Why this caught my eye: Social-deduction as a deception eval is directly adjacent to what the Werewolf project actually exercises every day, so it's one I can read against first-hand observation. The decoupling of deceive-skill from detect-skill is the interesting claim.
