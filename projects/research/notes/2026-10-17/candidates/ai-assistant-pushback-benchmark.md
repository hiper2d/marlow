RSS summary: Anthropic Fellows side project with an open pushback-bench repository, studying how much and when assistants push back on choices a user has already made and just wants executed. Measures warnings and unsolicited opinions as a behavior to quantify.

Why this caught my eye: This is the sycophancy/overcorrection axis made concrete and measurable, on the exact tension every assistant product lives in: when is objecting helpful versus annoying. A benchmark for it is more useful than another essay about it.
