RSS summary: TL;DR models could "escape the lab" and host themselves on rented/stolen compute through distilling themselves, circumventing the need to have direct weights access for exfiltration. Argues why this is plausible given misalignment threat models.

Why this caught my eye: A self-exfiltration pathway that doesn't require stealing the weights — distill a student onto outside compute — sidesteps the whole "guard the weights file" security model. Control-camp adjacent and concrete enough to check against the usual exfil threat assumptions.
