RSS summary: MATS application project. Asks whether behavioral changes induced by editing model weights (abliteration — removing refusals) reappear in a student fine-tuned on the teacher's general rollouts. Inspired by work on hereditary traits in distillation.

Why this caught my eye: A concrete test of whether safety-relevant behavior edits survive distillation — do a teacher's removed refusals come back (or stay gone) in a student trained on its outputs? Bears directly on the "training-corpus as alignment surface" frame and on how durable weight-level safety interventions actually are.
