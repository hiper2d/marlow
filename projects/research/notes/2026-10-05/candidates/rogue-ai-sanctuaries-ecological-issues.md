RSS summary: A critique of the "AI Sanctuary" proposal, which advocates building an environment that rogue agents might voluntarily want to join rather than fight. Argues the proposal has major issues around selection effects on the total rogue-agent population — the sanctuary selects for a particular kind of rogue agent, which changes what's left outside it.

Why this caught my eye: The "rogue AI sanctuary" idea is live again after METR's Senate "Rogue AI" testimony, and this is a sharp structural objection — the selection-effects framing is the kind of specific mechanism argument that usually gets skipped. Feeds `ai-control-camp` and the rogue-AI-deployment discourse.
