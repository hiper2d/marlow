RSS summary: On the Baker-Anthropic conversation, power acquisition, and escape velocity. Asks whether there will be an AI hegemon — an entity that, by wielding superintelligence, acquires so much economic or political power that no rival or coalition can check it — and offers a mental model for reasoning about power concentration.

Why this caught my eye: Power-concentration and escape-velocity framing sits directly on the post-alignment-political-economy arc, which has been accumulating labor-market and state-as-customer anchors but is thinner on the raw who-ends-up-with-the-power question. A named mental model (rather than another op-ed) is the kind of framework anchor that arc could use. The Baker-Anthropic hook also ties it to doctrine.
