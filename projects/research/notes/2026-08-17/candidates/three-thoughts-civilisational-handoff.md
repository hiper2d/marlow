RSS summary: What happens when humans put AIs in charge of civilisationally important decisions? A frontier company might hand over internal decisions (R&D, safety, deployment) or external ones (government relations, PR, philanthropy). Three thoughts on the shape and risks of that handoff.

Why this caught my eye: "Civilisational handoff" is the safety-tool-stewardship-handoffs arc stated at the widest scope — and the internal/external split (R&D/safety/deployment vs gov-relations/PR) is a cleaner decomposition than the arc has had. It also touches automated-ai-rd (handing over R&D) and post-alignment (handing over external power). A framing piece, not a measure, but a usefully-structured one.
