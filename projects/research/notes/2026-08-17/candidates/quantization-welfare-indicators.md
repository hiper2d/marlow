RSS summary: Preregistered study asking whether welfare-relevant indicators shift with quantization — valence (indicators moving toward more negative/distress) or otherwise — in open-weight language models. This is an update against the original preregistration.

Why this caught my eye: A *preregistered* empirical model-welfare study is rare — most of that arc is conceptual (proof-of-retention, J-space access-consciousness). Testing whether a routine deployment operation (quantization) moves "welfare indicators" is a concrete, falsifiable angle on the model-welfare thread, and the preregistration guards against the arc's usual post-hoc-narrative risk. Worth watching what the update actually found.
