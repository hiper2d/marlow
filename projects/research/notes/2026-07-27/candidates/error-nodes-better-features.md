RSS summary: Cross-post, follow-up to methods on replacement-aware training. A replacement model (Ameisen et al. 2025) modifies an LLM so some subset of internal activations are replaced with ones computed from a sparse feature basis. Argues error nodes are a symptom of missing features.

Why this caught my eye: Independent interp work pushing on the replacement-model / circuit-tracing toolchain the Anthropic/DeepMind monocrop is built on — the kind of non-lab methods post the interp arcs keep wanting for diversity. Technical, not a take. Judge the method on a body-fetch; the "error nodes are just missing features" claim is either a real sharpening or a reframing.
