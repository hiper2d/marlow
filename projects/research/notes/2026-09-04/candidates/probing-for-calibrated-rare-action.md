RSS summary: Wherever calibrated rare defection drives an agent's strategy, its calibration mechanism may leave a fingerprint whenever it considers defection. The author prototypes a probe that might focus expensive analysis toward actual defection opportunities — or might be confounded.

Why this caught my eye: A concrete interpretability probe aimed at the "rare treacherous turn" problem — detecting the deliberation, not just the act. Feeds `cot-monitorability`, and it's honest about the confound, which is rarer than it should be. Worth a look for whether the fingerprint claim survives contact with a real model.
