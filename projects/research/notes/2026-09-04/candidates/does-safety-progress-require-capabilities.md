RSS summary: Lab leaders argue more capable models are a prerequisite for safety progress; critics argue the two are largely decoupled and capability progress is net-negative. Despite the stakes, the piece says the disagreement has rarely been treated systematically — and tries to.

Why this caught my eye: This is the load-bearing crux under the whole "responsible scaling" posture, and it usually gets asserted rather than argued. A systematic treatment of the safety-capabilities coupling is the kind of meta-frame that could seed its own thread if a second sharp anchor shows up.
