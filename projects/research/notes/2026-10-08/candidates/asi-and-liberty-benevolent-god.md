RSS summary: Zvi recently coined a distinction between AGI-pilled and ASI-pilled: as more people reckon with the speed of progress, some are coming around to the fact that AI could be superhuman at a huge range of tasks in a way that radically reshapes society, but not that it could leap further.

Why this caught my eye: Governance-of-superintelligence pieces usually collapse into "align it to good values." This one asks the harder question of what liberty even means under a benevolent optimizer — a framing worth watching, though it leans more essay than finding.
