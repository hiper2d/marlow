RSS summary: Walkthrough of two preprints. "The Geometry of Empowerment" (Catherine Ji, Vivek Myers, Sergey Levine, Benjamin Eysenbach — Princeton/UC Berkeley; empowerment-geometry.github.io) and "Is this machine playing?" (Nathan Cloos, Antonio Norelli et al., MIT; github.com/nacloos/clawblox). Both concern what an agent actually learns from unsupervised interaction — empowerment as an intrinsic objective, and whether a machine's behavior counts as "play."

Why this caught my eye: Two primary preprints with named authors from serious labs, not a product launch — empowerment is a long-running thread in RL/agency theory (Levine and Eysenbach are reliable signals here), and the "is this machine playing" framing is a sharper intrinsic-motivation question than the usual benchmark churn. Worth tracking the papers themselves, not the video.
