RSS summary: A working-draft design for how future AI could work, aiming to ethically handle AIs that may be moral patients with meaningful preferences while keeping them corrigible. Explicitly framed as an early idea, not a finished proposal.

Why this caught my eye: It tries to hold corrigibility and model-welfare in the same frame — the two goals that most alignment writing treats as in tension. Sits at the intersection of `model-welfare-and-consciousness` and `alignment-target-definitions`. The "employee" metaphor is doing a lot of work and is worth pressure-testing.
