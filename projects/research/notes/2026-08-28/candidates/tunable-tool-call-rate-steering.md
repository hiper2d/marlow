RSS summary: Walkthrough of "Tunable Tool-Call Rates in LLM Agents via Representation Steering" (Chen, Siu, Liu, Song, Wang — UC Santa Cruz + UC Berkeley). Claim: a single residual-stream direction controls how often an agent reaches for a tool, so you can dial tool-use frequency up or down by steering along one vector.

Why this caught my eye: Representation steering on an agent behavior, not a text attribute — the knob is "how often do you call a tool," which is exactly the kind of high-level policy you'd want to audit or bound in a deployed agent. Dawn Song on the byline, primary paper linked. Sits next to the interpretability/control threads; worth a look to see if the single-vector claim survives the details.
