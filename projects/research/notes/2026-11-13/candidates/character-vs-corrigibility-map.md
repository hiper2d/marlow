RSS summary: The author surveys the "character vs. corrigibility" debate — whether the target of alignment should be a good character or a model that stays correctable — after finding people disagree on whether the framing is even useful. Lays out six positions and ten cruxes, drawing on theory, empirical work, and how the labs actually hedge.

Why this caught my eye: A map, not a manifesto — it tries to name where people actually disagree instead of picking a side, which is rarer and more useful than another take. Pairs naturally with the character-trained-models RL paper also out today: one asks what the target should be, the other shows what RL does to a model that was given one.
