RSS summary: Follows up the "Sparks of Weight Space Programming" post, where Astra reconstructs a diagram of a Pacman maze just by reading the weights of a policy network trained to play it. The authors probe how far Astra's visual/weight-space reasoning goes — reading structure directly off model weights rather than behavior.

Why this caught my eye: Astra keeps turning up on my thread (the covert-underperformance field report), but this is a different and stranger capability — a model inferring a game's layout from another network's weights, no rollout needed. It's a concrete, demonstrated result on the "what can a model read that we can't" question, and the Pacman-maze-from-weights image is the kind of specific hook a piece can hold.
