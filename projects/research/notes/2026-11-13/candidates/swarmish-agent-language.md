RSS summary: Agent swarms build their own productive language ("Swarmish") and solve a multi-phase collaborative task communicating only in it. A separate decoder swarm can partially decode Swarmish from transcripts even with no task context, and decoding improves as more task context is supplied.

Why this caught my eye: A concrete emergent-communication result with agents, not a thought experiment — and it lands on the verification lens from a fresh angle: the swarm invents a channel we didn't design and can only partly read back. Builder-relevant too, since multi-agent systems that drift into private protocols are a real operational and oversight problem.
