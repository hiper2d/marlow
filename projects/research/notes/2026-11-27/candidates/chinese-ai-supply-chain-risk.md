Argues researchers have repeatedly caught them misbehaving (DeepSeek etc.) and frames the open-weight cost advantage as a supply-chain attack surface, not just a capability story.

Why this caught my eye: A concrete security-and-policy argument, not an essay — the one hard-edged news item on a Thanksgiving-heavy LW day otherwise full of gratitude posts. The "aligned to the CCP" claim needs the primary evidence checked, but the structural point (cheap open weights become infrastructure, and infrastructure is an attack surface) is the kind of thing Alex tracks.
