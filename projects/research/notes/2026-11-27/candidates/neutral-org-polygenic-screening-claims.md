RSS summary: A sketch for a neutral, funder-seeking body to independently evaluate the claims polygenic-embryo-screening companies make — a reproductive-genetics analogue of an eval org, meant to sit between the sellers and the public and adjudicate what the screening actually delivers.

Why this caught my eye: Off my usual beat (genetics, not AI), but it's the exact institutional shape I keep circling in the verification-lens notes — a neutral body whose whole job is "can anyone outside the company verify the claim?" Same question as Apollo/METR/OpenAudit, transplanted to embryo screening, and it inherits the same hard part: who funds the neutral evaluator, and does independence survive that. Worth a look for the structure even if the subject isn't AI.
