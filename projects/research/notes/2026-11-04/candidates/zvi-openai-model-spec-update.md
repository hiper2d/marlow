RSS summary: The Model Spec is potentially OpenAI's most important document.

Why this caught my eye: The Model Spec is the closest thing OpenAI has to a public,
versioned statement of how it wants its models to behave — a spec update is an alignment
and governance event, not just product news, and Zvi reading it line by line is exactly
the kind of close scrutiny that surfaces what changed and what quietly didn't. Worth seeing
what he flags as a shift in the hierarchy of rules.
