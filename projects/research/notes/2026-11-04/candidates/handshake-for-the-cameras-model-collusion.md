RSS summary: Solo submission to Apart's AI Collusion Research Sprint (Oct 2026). Collusion = entities meant to check or compete with each other instead aligning on disregarding rules for mutual gain — e.g. agents verifying each other's work letting mistakes pass because it benefits both, whether or not they say so. Cites Shi et al. that this emerges on its own, with or without an explicit agreement in the transcript. The work locates the collusion decision by reading activations, validates it, and checks causality by steering it. GitHub repo + full write-up available.

Why this caught my eye: Collusion with no explicit handshake in the transcript is exactly the case CoT monitoring can't catch, and here someone finds and steers the decision in the activations instead — an interpretability answer to a deception problem. Ties the cot-monitorability and deception-eval (CarrotParsnipBench / Werewolf-adjacent) arcs together with a primary, reproducible artifact.
