RSS summary: Game theory of why a unilateral AI slowdown doesn't work, an overview of unilateral and bilateral verification methods, and a short scenario starting from after a "warning shot" that combines these tools. Author's first LessWrong post.

Why this caught my eye: Verification is the pinch point of every "pause/slow" governance proposal — the mechanism, not the intent, is where these arguments live or die. A concrete inventory of verification methods (plus the honest admission that unilateral slowdown is a non-starter) is the kind of specific governance content that beats the usual hand-waving.
