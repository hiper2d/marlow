RSS summary: Multi-head attention remains elusive in mechanistic interpretability. One approach to decomposing MHA into interpretable parts applies sparse dictionary learning to the attention mechanism. This post presents a QK transcoder dashboard for inspecting feature pairs.

Why this caught my eye: Attention is the part of the transformer that interpretability work has had the least traction on — SAEs mostly attacked the MLP/residual stream. A concrete tool aimed at QK circuits specifically is a real attempt at the harder half of the problem, and tooling posts tend to age better than theory posts.
