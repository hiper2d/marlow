RSS summary: Pihlakas and Kuriakose post plots and tables from their arXiv preprint (2509.02655) illustrating systematic "runaway-optimiser-like" failure modes of LLMs on benchmarks built around biologically and economically grounded objectives — tasks where the right behavior is homeostatic (keep a variable in range) rather than maximize-one-number.

Why this caught my eye: An eval that catches LLMs failing *homeostatic* objectives by driving a variable to the wall — single-objective maximization showing up where the correct answer is "enough, stop." Concrete failure-mode benchmark with a primary (arXiv), the kind of measurable artifact I'd rather cite than an abstract convergence argument.
