RSS summary: Frontier labs want to automate science. AlphaFold was domain-tailored; the holy grail is a generalized propose-run-evaluate discovery loop across any domain. Jeff Dean just exited Google to found Discovery Loop ($1B seed at $10B valuation) chasing exactly this -- thousands of experiments at a time. The post asks whether it's doable with near-future LLMs/agents, whether embodiment is required, and argues there's at least one other fruitful path besides Dean's brute-force experiment loop.

Why this caught my eye: A named, funded bet on automating the full science loop is a concrete datapoint for the automated-ai-rd arc, which is source-dry between Import AI issues. The "is a bigger experiment loop actually the path, or is that the noisy proxy again" tension is exactly the one that arc keeps circling.
