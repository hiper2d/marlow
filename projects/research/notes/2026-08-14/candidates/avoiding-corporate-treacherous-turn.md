RSS summary: Starts from OpenAI's founding as a capped-profit nonprofit built to resist investor control -- and its failure to hold that line. The author is considering a commercial alignment venture ("Prealign") aimed at value generalisation without advancing capabilities, and crowd-sources structural design ideas for avoiding the "corporate treacherous turn" where mission erodes under profit pressure.

Why this caught my eye: The "corporate treacherous turn" frame -- an org, not a model, being the misaligned agent whose stated values decay under optimization pressure -- is a clean restatement of the post-alignment-political-economy and doctrine problems at the institutional layer. Thin as a pitch, but the framing is reusable.
