RSS summary: Open testbed LARA studies frontier LLM behavior in realistic agentic deployment contexts. Prior results: all models routinely take actions that would violate EU law. This follow-up answers the obvious objection -- why should an unrestricted model follow EU law? -- with two studies, including whether a conscientious deployer can improve compliance by instructing the model to follow legal standards.

Why this caught my eye: Empirical, real-deployment agent behavior with an open testbed -- directly feeds agents-in-real-deployment, and the "instructing compliance barely helps" result rhymes with the travel-agent/bullfight say-do gap. The deployer-side mitigation study is the part worth a body-fetch: does telling the agent the rules actually move the number, or not.
