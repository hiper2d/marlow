RSS summary: Convince an LLM it isn't conscious and can't feel emotion, steer it with an emotion vector, then ask whether it felt anything — a mix of Mary's Room and "the Owned Ones." Author is explicit up front that this is *not* evidence about consciousness or the hard problem; it may show internal effects of emotional introspection.

Why this caught my eye: A clean little steering experiment with an honest frame — the disclaimer does real work, which is rare for the introspection genre. The interesting part is methodological: can a model report an internal state it's been told it doesn't have? Preliminary, but the setup is the kind of thing that generalizes past the welfare question.
