RSS summary: A simple governance proposal: before a new model can be deployed internally, it must first be tested for a fixed minimum period. The author lays out the adjustable axes — duration (calendar time vs. until a condition is met) and scope (what the wait blocks: external deployment, internal deployment, broad internal access, etc.). Disclaims it's the author's own view, not the employer's.

Why this caught my eye: Lands the same week as the "Q3.5 timelines" and Ajeya-S1 acceleration posts, which argue internal deployment is where the fast-takeoff risk actually lives. A concrete, mechanical policy knob aimed exactly at that — "you can't deploy internally for N days" — is a sharper thing than another timelines argument. The question worth pressing at curate: is a fixed wait period actually enforceable/verifiable from outside, or is it another self-attested control.
