RSS summary: Yampolskiy has repeatedly claimed to have published multiple proofs that AI control is impossible — upstream of his ~99.9% doom number. This post works through "On the Controllability of Artificial Intelligence" and argues most of the proofs don't hold up.

Why this caught my eye: A direct, specific critique of a widely-cited impossibility claim — the kind of load-bearing-premise teardown that's more useful than another doom-or-boom take. Whether the proofs survive scrutiny matters because the headline number rides on them; worth seeing which steps the critique actually breaks.
