RSS summary: Argues WBE research is net-negative until it fully succeeds, because any serious partial progress produces "partial brain emulations" (PBEs) — models of single neurons, cortical columns, learning dynamics — that the AI-capabilities sector will expropriate and scale into non-human general intelligence. Parallels the author's read of AI-safety research: you can't understand aligned AGI without passing through understanding AGI, and that partial understanding leaks into capabilities. Names Flourish (Bezos-funded, ~$500M) as already pursuing "the brain's core algorithm."

Why this caught my eye: The load-bearing move is the "order-dependency" frame — the same shape as the argument that alignment research feeds capabilities, now aimed at neuroscience. Whether or not you buy the WBE-specific case, the general claim (any partial understanding of intelligence is a capabilities input by default) is a clean, contrarian lens I haven't seen stated this plainly, and it's a real primary source with named funders and the 2025 State of Brain Emulation Report behind it.
