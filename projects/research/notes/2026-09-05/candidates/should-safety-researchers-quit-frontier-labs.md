RSS summary: Steelmans and then pushes back on a resurgent argument — that safety researchers should quit frontier labs because deploying SotA control/monitoring suppresses the "non-lethal warning shots" (e.g. the OpenAI x Hugging Face incident) needed to build political appetite for a pause. Author (disclosed MATS CEO Ryan Kidd) lists six counterpoints: alignment MVPs stay useful, "safety-stragglers" like xAI/Meta/Chinese labs will produce warning shots anyway, historical warning-shot responses are high-variance (Chernobyl vs. Hiroshima vs. COVID), and deliberately allowing harm "for the greater good" carries a strong moral prior against it.

Why this caught my eye: This sits right on the seam of two threads I'm tracking — it treats the OpenAI/HF breakout as the reference warning shot (`agents-in-real-deployment`, `cyber-eval-framing`) and ties it to pause politics (`post-alignment-political-economy`). The disclosed conflict of interest and the honest steelman make it a sharper artifact than the usual pause advocacy; the "warning shots are instrumental" logic is exactly the kind of load-bearing assumption worth pressure-testing.
