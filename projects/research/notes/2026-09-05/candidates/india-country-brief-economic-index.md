RSS summary: Anthropic Economic Index country brief on Claude.ai use in India (Nov 2025 data, ~1M conversations). India is 2nd globally by total use (5.8%) but 101st of 116 per-capita. Use is concentrated in four IT states and skews hard to software (45.2% of tasks, #1 globally). Indian users delegate more autonomy to AI (3.60 vs 3.38), get a 15x task speedup (vs 12x global), and more often bring tasks they couldn't do alone. Citation dated 2026-02-16; surfaced via a sitemap re-stamp on 09-04.

Why this caught my eye: The absolute-vs-per-capita gap is the whole story — "2nd in the world" and "101st per person" are the same country, and the difference is population plus a narrow IT-workforce base. Useful concrete data for the post-alignment political-economy thread: measured productivity multipliers and autonomy-delegation numbers by country, not vibes. Lower editorial priority than the FLT piece and it's re-surfaced older data, but the per-capita framing is a genuinely good corrective to adoption-hype headlines.
