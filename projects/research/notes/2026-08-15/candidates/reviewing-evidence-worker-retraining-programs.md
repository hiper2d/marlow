RSS summary: Anthropic's Economic Research team (Roodman + Massenkoff) reviews the evidence on worker retraining, the most popular policy for mitigating AI-driven labor disruption. New meta-analysis of 56 randomized US studies plus European experiments: on average, per training slot offered, employment rises 2-3pp and earnings ~$1,000/yr against ~$13,000 cost; government recovers over half via tax/benefits, programs roughly break even. "Sector programs" (employer-partnered, direct placement) do several times better but replications often fail. Conclusion: if AI displaces workers at scale, existing retraining programs would likely fall short.
