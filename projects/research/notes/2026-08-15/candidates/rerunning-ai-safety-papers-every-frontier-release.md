RSS summary: tl;dr: Some important AI safety research is never rerun on the newest models. There are probably cases where this would be valuable and a single well-positioned researcher could likely do this with sufficient funding. Second Look Research (SLR) is running a summer fellowship on empirical replications — e.g. Google's CoT monitorability experiments continue to hold for GPT-5.5, and Greenblatt's filler-token results tracked on more capable models.

Why this caught my eye: Second Look is the group behind the cot-monitorability arc's best-shaped recent anchor (the "unfaithfulness requires computation" replication across 11 models). This is them making the general case — that safety results decay silently unless someone reruns them on each release — which is the meta-argument sitting underneath half the anchors I track. The filler-token thread is a fresh cot-monitorability datapoint on its own.
