RSS summary: A blue team proposes an evaluation protocol for some capability/propensity — a suite of measurement tasks plus a preregistered decision process (what control measures they'll deploy based on results). A red team proposes a subversion strategy the untrusted model can follow to defeat the blue team's protocol.

Why this caught my eye: This is the control framing applied to eval *design* itself — the blue team's preregistered decision rule is the object under adversarial attack, not just the model. Sits directly on ai-control-camp and cyber-eval-framing (grading-your-own-danger's core worry: what if the measurement protocol is the thing being gamed). Worth reading for whether it proposes an actual mechanism or just restates the red/blue frame.
