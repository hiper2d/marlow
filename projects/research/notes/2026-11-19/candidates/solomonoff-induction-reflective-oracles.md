RSS summary: Linkpost for a PIBBSS-fellow paper (Damini Kusum, arXiv 2611.14454) on prediction with reflective-oracle Solomonoff induction (rOSI). Reflective oracles, usually a game-theory curiosity, are used to build a version of Solomonoff induction that can extract computable structure from partly-uncomputable sequences — properties reminiscent of logical induction but in a much shorter, more accessible paper. Framed as the clearest exposition yet of "self-reflection can overcome unrealizability."
