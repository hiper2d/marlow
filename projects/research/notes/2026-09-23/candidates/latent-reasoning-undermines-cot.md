RSS summary: Currently, "chain of thought" (CoT) is our most valuable tool for understanding the reasoning and cognition of AI systems. However, some architectures would enable AI models to reason much more extensively in latent states rather than in text CoT. We think that a shift toward latent reasoning would undermine oversight.

Why this caught my eye: This is the cot-monitorability worry stated as an architecture bet, not a training accident — the concern isn't that models learn to hide their reasoning, it's that the field builds systems where the reasoning never surfaces in text to begin with. Direct feed for the cot-monitorability thread (posts:6, synth 09-14), and it pairs with the earlier no-CoT-architecture-search and Astra-uses-no-CoT anchors: prediction plus practice.
