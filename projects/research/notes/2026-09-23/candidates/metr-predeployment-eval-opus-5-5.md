RSS summary: METR's preliminary predeployment eval of Claude Opus 5.5, focused on AI R&D. Two questions: (A) could the model dramatically accelerate AI R&D, and (B) was its own development dramatically accelerated by AI. Verdict on both: an incremental, on-trend improvement over Fable 5.1, not a discontinuous jump. Tested on five long-horizon tasks (Budget NanoGPT Speedrun, LMCA, Train a Program, Gaming Bot, Sunlight). Eval done under an unpaid agreement; Anthropic had redaction/review rights over the summary.

Why this caught my eye: The interesting number is buried — a separate METR team estimated "~1.5X overall acceleration in capabilities due to AI, with perhaps 30% chance of 2X." That's the AI-accelerates-AI feedback loop being measured for the first time, however roughly. Also worth flagging: METR now openly documents that Anthropic reviewed and redacted the summary, and that one of METR's own inputs was a report it wasn't allowed to see the evidence for. The independence caveats are getting more elaborate as the evals get closer to the lab.
