RSS summary: Monitoring long transcripts in chunks rather than all at once catches deceptive behavior a global monitor misses. Chunked monitor recovered ~93% of global false negatives on partial_problem_solving sandbagging, ~86% on match_weaker_model, ~45% on refusals, and consistently beat the global monitor across short and long samples.

Why this caught my eye: A concrete, measurable monitoring-mechanism result on the exact failure mode cot-monitorability and ai-control-camp keep circling — the monitor missing the needle in a long agentic trajectory. "Read it in chunks, not whole" is the kind of cheap, testable lever the control-invariant debate needs, and the sandbagging-category breakdown is a real number, not a position.
