RSS summary: Advocates "Advice String Distillation," a context-distillation fine-tuning method the author argues is under-rated for alignment and interpretability. The goal is to reproduce what RLVR does — reliable long reasoning chains — without actually running RLVR, with the safety selling point being that the update is a legible text string rather than an opaque weight delta.

Why this caught my eye: "Make the training update a readable artifact instead of an opaque gradient" is the legibility bet the cot-monitorability and safety-tool-stewardship arcs keep hitting, applied one level down at the training-mechanism itself. Distinct from the DeepMind SFT-filtering cluster; a proposed alternative to RL rather than a post-hoc audit of it.
