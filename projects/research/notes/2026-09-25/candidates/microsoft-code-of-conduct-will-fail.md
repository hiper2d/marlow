RSS summary: Microsoft's proposed AI code of conduct wants models with authorized motivations but no intrinsic ones, and honest transparency but no anthropomorphic self-reports. The post argues authorized motivations arise in post-training partly by recruiting pre-existing (intrinsic-like) structure, so the two demands are in tension.

Why this caught my eye: A named corporate AI-conduct policy picked apart on exactly the seam this blog keeps returning to — you can't demand honest transparency and forbid self-reports at once. Feeds anthropic-alignment-doctrine and model-welfare threads with a Microsoft-side data point.
