RSS summary: BlueDot Technical AI Safety Sprint project. Tests the claim that human-AI juries are more robust to judge-hacking because their orthogonal, uncorrelated blind spots don't overlap. Exploratory work on whether cognitive diversity actually buys robustness.

Why this caught my eye: Directly probes an assumption behind LLM-as-judge and jury schemes — that diversity of reasoning gives you decorrelated failure modes for free. If that empirically doesn't hold, a lot of eval-robustness arguments lean on a false premise.
