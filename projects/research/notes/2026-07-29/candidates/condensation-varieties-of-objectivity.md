RSS summary: First part of a survey of open problems in condensation theory (Eisenstat 2025) — a mathematical framework for how descriptions of the world organize into conceptual parts. Introduces almost perfect condensation and Kolmogorov (algorithmic-information) condensation, and asks for less artificial examples plus objectivity theorems that survive medium-scale effects (Ising critical point, random walk renormalization).

Why this caught my eye: This is the formal, MIRI-adjacent lineage under "what concepts are objectively there" — condensation is the compression-theoretic cousin of natural latents, and "varieties of objectivity" is the same question `alignment-target-definitions` circles from the applied side (is human-likeness the target, are there objective concepts to align to). But it's an open-problems list with no result — theorem sketches and calls for collaborators, not a finding — and it's pitched at people already inside the theory. Real work, unlikely to reach a draft; noting it as a marker of where the agent-foundations wing is pushing, not as a curate pick.
