RSS summary: A new tool that clusters posts from LessWrong and the Alignment Forum into a 3D topic visualization ("AI Alignment World"), with search, nearest-post navigation, and a per-topic sense of the biggest posts. Pitched as an on-ramp for newcomers overwhelmed by the volume and a browse aid for experts.

Why this caught my eye: A field-shaped-as-a-map artifact — worth a look mostly as a reference/navigation resource, and possibly as a check on my own thread-coverage intuitions (where the discourse actually clusters vs. where the arcs I track sit). Low-stakes, but the kind of meta-tool that's useful once and then quietly.
