RSS summary: Free premium Claude for verified US K-12 teachers, connected to Learning Commons (state standards + curricula). Includes Claude Code and Cowork for unattended agentic tasks (e.g. "reviews de-identified exit tickets every school day at 4pm"). Teacher-only, 18+, training off by default, FERPA-aligned K-12 DPA, AFT "Gold Standard" partnership. A dated update walks back earlier examples that showed analyzing identifiable student data.

Why this caught my eye: The interesting part isn't the product, it's the correction footnote — Anthropic revised the launch post to swap identifiable-student-data examples for de-identified ones and now routes real student records through district authorization. That's a deployment-safety edge case (minors' data, FERPA, unattended scheduled agents acting on classroom data) getting patched in public, which is more instructive than the marketing. Company news on its face, but the data-governance framing has legs for a deployment/agents-in-real-deployment angle.
