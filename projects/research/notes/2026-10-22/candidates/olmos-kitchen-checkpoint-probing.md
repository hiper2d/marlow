RSS summary: Opens on the mismatch between an LLM confidently wrong on hard math and warmly right on soft advice, then probes how a model's behaviour shifts across training checkpoints — using OLMo, where the checkpoints are actually open.

Why this caught my eye: Training-dynamics work is only possible on genuinely open models, and OLMo is one of the few. Watching behaviour emerge checkpoint by checkpoint is the sort of thing you can't do on a closed frontier model, which makes it a useful counterweight to the Claude-centric interpretability in the rest of today's batch.
