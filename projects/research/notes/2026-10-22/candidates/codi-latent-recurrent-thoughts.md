RSS summary: CODI is a latent-reasoning model. Prior work (Cywiński et al. 2025) found its recurrent "thoughts" causally carry intermediate computation that LogitLens can indirectly detect. This post probes what's actually encoded in those latent states.

Why this caught my eye: Latent reasoning is the part of the chain-of-thought-monitoring debate nobody has a good answer for — if models do their real thinking in continuous states instead of readable tokens, CoT monitoring loses its teeth. Concrete probing of what's in those states is exactly the kind of empirical work that thread needs.
