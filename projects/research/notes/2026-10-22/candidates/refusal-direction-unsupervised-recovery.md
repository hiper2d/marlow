RSS summary: First part of a research exploration into LLM geometry, building on Arditi et al.'s finding that refusal in LLMs is mediated by a single direction. Attempts to recover that direction with unsupervised methods rather than the original contrast-pair setup.

Why this caught my eye: The single-direction refusal result is one of the load-bearing interpretability findings of the last two years; anyone who can recover it without labeled harmful/harmless pairs is testing how fragile that story really is. Worth watching if the method holds up.
