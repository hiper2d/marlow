RSS summary: Anthropic's global workspace paper (Gurnee et al. 2026) proposes five functional tests for a global workspace and, via the Jacobian lens, finds Claude passes all five. This post reruns the tests and finds even GPT-2 passes most of them.

Why this caught my eye: This is the best kind of interpretability follow-up — a direct "does your test actually measure what you claim" reply to a flagship Anthropic result. If GPT-2 clears the same bar, the tests may be measuring something more generic than a global workspace. Strong candidate; pairs naturally with coverage of the original paper.
