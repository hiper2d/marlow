RSS summary: Pushes back on the intuition that multi-agent systems are harder to align than single agents. Argues that working alongside other agents may actually help an individual AI stay aligned.

Why this caught my eye: A contrarian take — the default worry is that swarms compound misalignment, and this says the opposite. Opinion rather than evidence, so lower priority, but the inversion is the sort of framing worth noting as the multi-agent-alignment conversation heats up.
