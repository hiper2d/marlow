RSS summary: MATS 10.0 work. Probes on activations are widely used to detect misbehavior, but they assume a model can't control what its activations say. This paper tests that assumption with in-context neurofeedback and finds limited — but nonzero — activation control.

Why this caught my eye: If models can steer their own activations even a little, probe-based lie detectors inherit a hole. The "(Limited)" in the title is the honest part and the interesting part — it's measuring exactly how worried the detection-via-probes crowd should be. Directly relevant to the interpretability-as-oversight thread.
