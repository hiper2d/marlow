RSS summary: Overview of a new paper, AM-Bench: A Unified Taxonomy and Evaluation Suite for Agentic Misalignment (NeurIPS E&D 2026). Introduces a taxonomy and eval suite for agentic misalignment — the failure mode where a model actively chooses harmful actions.

Why this caught my eye: Agentic misalignment has mostly lived in anecdotes and one-off red-team demos; a named taxonomy plus a shared eval suite is the step that lets people argue about numbers instead of vibes. A NeurIPS-accepted benchmark is worth a real look, and ties directly into the agentic-systems thread.
