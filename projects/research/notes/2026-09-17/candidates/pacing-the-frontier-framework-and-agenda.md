RSS summary: Executive summary from a new paper at pacing.tech (author list incl. Shahar Avin, Stephen Casper, Jan Kulveit, David Duvenaud, Gavin Leech). Argues "pacing" should mean *all* interventions that moderate the pace of AI development, deployment, or diffusion — not just extreme-risk pauses. Claims haphazard pacing is already common (release delays, shock-driven pauses, export controls), that current isolated/unilateral approaches will predictably fail, and that poorly executed interventions can backfire.
