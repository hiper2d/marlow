RSS summary: tl;dr: people should understand and think hard about the problems they work on. We've observed that those who work in AI safety (ourselves included) often rely on concerning heuristics when choosing what to work on. Running a conference is probably good, doing pragmatic alignment research might be good.

Why this caught my eye: A field-hygiene piece arguing safety researchers pick problems on bad heuristics — the "is this legibly good" trap rather than "does this reduce risk." Meta rather than technical, but it names a real failure mode in how the field allocates attention, which is worth watching against the roadmap-style posts landing the same week.
