RSS summary: Asks whether ensuring AI systems are safe should be approached like an engineer or a scientist — whether we need deep fundamental truths about how intelligence works, or can apply battle-tested engineering methods. Argues safety cases inherit an unresolved agent-foundations problem.

Why this caught my eye: Directly on the safety-case / verification beat — a safety case is a structured argument that a system is safe, and this piece says the argument rests on foundations nobody has. That's the "who verifies the verifier" question one level deeper: the method we'd use to certify safety has a hole at its base.
