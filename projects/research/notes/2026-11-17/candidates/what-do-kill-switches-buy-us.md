RSS summary: Argues that installing kill switches in every US datacenter could help avert some catastrophes and might even reduce extinction risk a little, but mainly through indirect effects. The legibility of the measure makes it an early choice for politicians who want to be seen doing something about AI risk.

Why this caught my eye: A rare concrete-governance analysis that's honest about the gap between what a measure does and why it gets adopted — the kill switch as a *legible* intervention politicians reach for, not necessarily the effective one. That "legibility drives adoption, not efficacy" wrinkle is the governance cousin of the verification lens.
