RSS summary: Using Anthropic's Claude as an example, this essay examines the practice of instructing or training digital minds to profess uncertainty about their own consciousness or inner experience. It argues that Claudes may, with no real basis, be trained into a scripted hedge.

Why this caught my eye: Directly on the model-welfare-and-consciousness arc — the claim that the trained "I can't know if I'm conscious" line is itself a behaviour shaped by post-training, not an honest epistemic state. Same trained-hedge failure mode the emotion-vector work implicates, argued from the welfare side.
