RSS summary: tldr: There seems to be a dynamic closely related to instrumental convergence that occurs when many agents with diverse goals interact. Epistemic status: there is a version that is trivial and obvious, and a version that is empirically false, but somewhere in between is a useful concept.

Why this caught my eye: Instrumental convergence is usually argued about a single agent; extending it to a crowd of heterogeneous agents is the right question as multi-agent deployments become the norm. The author's own hedge (trivial-or-false, useful in between) is honest, which makes it worth a read even if it doesn't land.
