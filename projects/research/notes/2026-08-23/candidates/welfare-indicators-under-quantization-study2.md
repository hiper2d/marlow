RSS summary: A higher-quality preregistration for the next study in a series originally described in "Does post-training quantization change welfare-relevant indicators in open-weight language models?" The author tries to avoid claims not supportable from Study 1 and to look at representational counterparts.

Why this caught my eye: Preregistered, open-weight, and asking whether a purely engineering step (quantization) shifts welfare-relevant signals — an unusually falsifiable corner of the model-welfare arc, which mostly trades in interpretation. The preregistration itself is the credibility move.
