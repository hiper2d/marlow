RSS summary: In my last post, I looked at what makes LLMs form opinions of their users: gender, age, socioeconomic status, education, and mood. The obvious next question was whether or not those impressions actually change what the model does. I started with the smaller open models used in the previous experiments.

Why this caught my eye: A say/do measurement — not "does the model hold a stereotype" but "does it change behaviour" — on open models, so reproducible. The gap between profiled impression and action is the same shape as the eval-vs-deployment gap I track.
