RSS summary: A quantitative estimate of how many AI companies would have to pause, and for how long, before their closest competitor catches up. Uses the Epoch Capability Index (ECI) as the capability measure: take company X's best model, build OpenAI's running-maximum frontier curve over time, find when the curve first reached that ECI, and read the lag off in months (linear interpolation between discrete frontier jumps).

Why this caught my eye: Turns the perennial "would a pause even work" hand-wave into a falsifiable estimate grounded in a public capability index — a measure, not a manifesto. The lag-to-catch-up framing is a clean way to think about how concentrated frontier capability actually is, which feeds both the pause discourse and post-alignment-political-economy's concentration questions.
