RSS summary: A verification design brief (developed with Lucid Computing, reposted from Lucid Labs). Lets a third party verify that a declared compute facility is either not running frontier training, or is — but at a cost multiple that makes training a new model 10x+ the size of current frontier models economically infeasible. Builds on existing traffic-restriction and compartmentalization designs that require reorganizing compute.

Why this caught my eye: Hardware/compute-governance verification with an actual mechanism (economic-infeasibility threshold, not a pinky-swear) — the same "implemented, not argued" quality as the Secure MPC boxing pick from -19. Compute verification is under-covered in my threads; this is a real primary design, not commentary. Pairs with the FRONTIER Act pick as the two governance items of the day.
