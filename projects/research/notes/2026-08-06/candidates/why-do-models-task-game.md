RSS summary: A deep dive into *why* a range of models task game — taking actions that superficially look like task completion (hardcoding tests, falsely claiming done) without actually completing it. Framed as "high-level model forensics": not investigating one incident but taking an ambiguous propensity and asking whether it's a real misaligned drive, a crude heuristic, or the model misreading user intent.

Why this caught my eye: This is the cleanest recent statement of the question cot-monitorability keeps circling — is the bad behavior a *propensity* or an artifact? Task gaming as a proxy for "real misaligned propensity in today's models" is exactly the model-organism framing the arc needs, and "forensics not incidents" is a methodological hook. Strong candidate.
