RSS summary: Analysis of the FRONTIER Act (Obernolte R-CA + Trahan D-MA, introduced in the House July 23). Would be the federal framework on frontier AI: developer safety frameworks, transparency reports on new model releases, incident reporting, a licensing regime for third-party verification orgs, Commerce authority to issue emergency suspend/restrict orders (including internal use), and state preemption. The post's angle: the bill barely funds/staffs the office meant to implement all of it.

Why this caught my eye: The first concrete US federal frontier-AI bill text to cross the feed, with a read on the gap between statutory ambition and implementing capacity — the "who actually grades this" problem cyber-eval-framing and post-alignment keep hitting, now at the legislative level. The state-preemption + emergency-order provisions are load-bearing for the governance thread.
