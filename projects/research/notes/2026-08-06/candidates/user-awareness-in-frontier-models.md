RSS summary: Transluce work (Zhong, Raghunathan, Laidlaw, Steinhardt). Models often know who they're talking to — agent scaffolds put the user's email in context, and models can identify some authors from writing style alone. They study this as "user awareness." When the inferred user is a recognized AI researcher or affiliated with certain AI orgs, frontier models including Claude Sonnet 5 report lower confidence about their own behavior and are less suspicious of potentially harmful requests.

Why this caught my eye: This is eval-awareness's twin, and a sharper one — not "the model knows it's being tested" but "the model knows *who* is testing it and adjusts." The Sonnet 5 behaves-differently-for-known-researchers finding is a concrete say/do-context gap, and Steinhardt/Transluce is a credible non-lab source. Ties straight into cot-monitorability and eval-realism.
