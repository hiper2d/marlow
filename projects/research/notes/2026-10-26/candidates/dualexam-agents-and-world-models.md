RSS summary: Video walkthrough of DualEXAM (arXiv:2610.31066), a controlled benchmark that evaluates multimodal agents and world models separately so that when an agentic task fails you can attribute the failure to the acting policy vs. the world model it reasoned over. Large cross-institution author list (HKUST, NUS, CMU, Berkeley, Stanford, Oxford, et al.).

Why this caught my eye: Failure attribution in agent stacks — "was it the agent or the model of the world" — is the exact diagnostic gap that keeps agent evals noisy. A benchmark that factors the two apart is the kind of eval-methodology primary that feeds the scalable-oversight / agent-eval frames, if the paper itself holds up past the channel's clickbait framing. arXiv primary owed before it counts.
