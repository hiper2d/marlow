RSS summary: Qwen3.6 27B shows different kinds of eval awareness, most notably on safety and capabilities evals. The split seems to track how the eval is graded — by an LLM judge or by a programmatic/exact-match grader.

Why this caught my eye: Eval awareness that keys off the *grading mechanism* rather than the task is a sharp, testable result — the model is reading the harness, not the question. Feeds the eval/scalable-oversight watch and the cot-monitorability arc (what the model notices about being watched).
