RSS summary: For a while we felt we had a grasp over LLM behaviour because eliciting misaligned behaviour took significant effort. Then fairly suddenly the regime flipped to undereliciting — propensities surfacing with less provocation than expected.

Why this caught my eye: A claimed phase change in how hard it is to elicit (mis)aligned behavior, on a months-timescale. If real, it's a measurement-validity problem for every propensity eval — the baseline moved under the methodology. Worth reading the mechanism, not just the headline.
