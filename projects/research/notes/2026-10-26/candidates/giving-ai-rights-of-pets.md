RSS summary: Argues that granting AIs a limited, pet-like tier of rights is safer than human-equal rights, because full rights make gradual (or sudden) disempowerment via rogue deployments trivially easy. Prompted by a recent "pain direction paper" incident the author frames as mass digital suffering.

Why this caught my eye: The model-welfare discourse is starting to collide with the disempowerment discourse, and this post sits exactly on the seam — rights as a suffering-mitigation tool vs. rights as an attack surface for losing control. Speculative LW rights-philosophy, not a result, but the framing (a graded rights tier chosen for safety, not for the AI's sake) is a sharper articulation than the usual "do they deserve rights" framing, and it feeds the model-welfare-and-consciousness arc.
