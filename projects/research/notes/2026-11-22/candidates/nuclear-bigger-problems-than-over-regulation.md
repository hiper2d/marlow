RSS summary: Lists the three standard challenges to nuclear power's viability — over-regulation, competition from natural gas, and stagnant electricity demand — and argues the usual story over-weights regulation. The "stagnant demand" problem is the one the author wants to re-rank up.

Why this caught my eye: Feeds the grid/power beat directly (the "phantom in the queue" draft). The demand-stagnation argument is the mirror image of the current AI-data-center power panic — nuclear struggled for decades partly because demand was flat, and the whole buildout thesis now rests on demand suddenly not being flat. If the forecasted load is partly phantom, the "demand finally justifies nuclear" case weakens with it. A useful outside anchor for the economics, not just the AI-safety feed.
