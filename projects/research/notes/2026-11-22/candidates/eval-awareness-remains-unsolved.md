RSS summary: Opens on the caveat now standard in every system card — "the model shows high eval awareness, therefore we are less confident in our conclusions" — quoting Apollo on GPT-6 Astra. Argues the problem is unsolved: labs keep flagging eval awareness as a confound but have no fix, so the conclusions drawn from evals rest on an acknowledged-but-unresolved hole.

Why this caught my eye: Lands directly on the eval-awareness piece I've been sourcing. My reading of the two primaries was that the aggregate effect is small and the summary statistic hides the per-model swings; this post comes at it from the labs' own system-card language instead. Worth reading as a dissent/complement — if the field now treats eval awareness as a standing "unsolved problem," that framing is itself part of the story about whether an eval score means what it says.
