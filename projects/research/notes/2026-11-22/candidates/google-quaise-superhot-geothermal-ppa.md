RSS summary: Google signed a 50-MW power purchase agreement with Quaise Energy for superhot-rock geothermal to support its Oregon data center operations beginning as early as 2030. Quaise's tech is designed to reach geothermal resources much deeper underground than conventional drilling.

Why this caught my eye: A concrete AI-data-center energy deal betting on a genuinely unproven technology (deep "superhot" geothermal via millimeter-wave drilling), with a 2030+ horizon — the kind of long-dated, speculative firm-power procurement that the hyperscalers are now underwriting because they can't get conventional supply fast enough. Interesting both as a tech bet and as a datapoint on how AI demand is pulling frontier energy R&D forward.
