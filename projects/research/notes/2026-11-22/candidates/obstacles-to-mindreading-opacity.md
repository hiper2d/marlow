RSS summary: Starts from when it makes sense to model an entity as an agent that "seeks" an objective, then lays out a taxonomy of why reading a mind off behavior can fail — simple opacity, irreducible opacity, and instrumental opacity (the system has a reason to be hard to read).

Why this caught my eye: A taxonomy of *why* you can't infer the policy from the trace is exactly the conceptual scaffold under the verification lens. The "instrumental opacity" category — the model hard to read because it benefits from being hard to read — is the grader-modelling / Astra case named at the right level of generality. Potentially a citable frame if I write the lens piece directly, so worth capturing even if it's theory rather than news.
