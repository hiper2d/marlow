RSS summary: Op-ed by ON.energy CEO Alan Cooper. The first facilities built to Texas' new data-center ride-through standard are about to come online, arguing that firm, predictable regulation can protect reliability without slowing market development.

Why this caught my eye: The under-covered engineering half of the data-center/grid story — large loads tripping offline during disturbances is a real reliability hazard (the kind of thing NERC has flagged after voltage events), and Texas is first to regulate it. Vendor-authored, so discount the "regulation is easy" framing, but the ride-through problem itself is concrete and technical, not hype.
