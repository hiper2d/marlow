RSS summary: TARA capstone, code on GitHub. Problem: superposition packs many concepts into small latent spaces via almost-orthogonal feature vectors; that entanglement makes models opaque and breaks safety interventions (concept erasure, activation steering) that need clean, isolated directions. SONI proposes noise injection to selectively orthogonalise features.

Why this caught my eye: Directly downstream of the steering-directions-as-explanations-not-handles negative result on the safety-tool-stewardship arc — a proposed *mechanism* for the entanglement that makes steering/erasure fail, from an independent (non-lab) capstone. If it works it's a handle where the arc has been collecting handles-that-aren't. Judge the method, not the pitch.
