RSS summary: Anthropic commits $200M to fund external research on preparing the economy for AI disruption, across five priorities: firm/workplace-level impact on workers, navigating transitions (retraining, apprenticeships), modernizing income support (UI reform, longer-duration unconditional income pilots), building worker equity stakes before disruption (pre-distributive capital accounts, AI-sector dividends, token/compute taxes), and evidence on public investment (guaranteed-jobs pilots, place-based interventions). Grants $5-30M, RCTs and "ambitious pilots," nothing below $1M. An evolution of the year-old Economic Futures program, tied to Anthropic's June Economic Policy Framework.
