RSS summary: Announces v0 of Orbit, a framework for multi-agent safety and security evaluations, completed under MATS 9 (supervised by Christian Schroeder de Witt) and now supported by the Cooperative AI Foundation. Open repo.

Why this caught my eye: Multi-agent *security* evals as a named artifact, not another single-agent obstacle course — sits at the agents-in-real-deployment / cyber-eval-framing seam where the "trajectory is what matters" gap keeps getting named but not tooled. Cooperative AI Foundation backing gives it legs. Judge on whether it measures anything the existing single-agent evals miss.
