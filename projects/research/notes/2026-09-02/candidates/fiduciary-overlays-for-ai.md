RSS summary: On fiduciary AI — systems that hold duties of care and loyalty to their user, drawing the analogy to doctors, lawyers, and financial advisors as an antidote to the conflict of interest between AI provider and user. Comes with an RFP opportunity attached.

Why this caught my eye: A concrete framing of the "who does the model actually work for" question that keeps surfacing in the alignment-target and model-welfare threads. The provider-vs-user conflict of interest is real and under-discussed relative to capability-risk stories; the fiduciary framing gives it a legal precedent to hang on. Funding attached makes it more than a think-piece.
