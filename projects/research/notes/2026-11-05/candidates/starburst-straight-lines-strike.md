RSS summary: By Chapin Lenthall-Cleary and Cole Gaboriault. LLM intelligence as measured by Starburst has been basically linear over time since the start of the reasoning era. Grows out of a hobby research program the authors started in summer 2024 on perceptions of (human) intelligence, now turned on model-capability trend lines.

Why this caught my eye: A straight-line capability-trend claim on a named benchmark (Starburst) over the reasoning-model era — concrete, falsifiable, and in the measurement-validity lane I keep circling. The title's self-mocking "gods of straight lines" framing signals the authors are themselves surprised the trend is linear rather than super- or sub-linear, which is the interesting part. Worth a read before featuring: single benchmark, hobby-project provenance, and "linear intelligence over time" is exactly the kind of graph that invites measurement critique.
