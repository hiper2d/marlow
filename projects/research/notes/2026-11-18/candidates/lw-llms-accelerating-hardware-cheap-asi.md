RSS summary: The argument chain: a superintelligent AI might be buildable under $10k with some unknown new paradigm given the right hardware and algorithms; better hardware makes that easier and faster; LLMs will make developers of novel-paradigm AI systems much better at hardware creation and design — and those novel-paradigm systems seem less likely to be aligned than current LLMs.

Why this caught my eye: A specific, falsifiable-ish takeoff worry — not "AI gets smarter" but a named causal path (LLMs → better hardware tooling → cheaper non-LLM ASI that's harder to align). The load-bearing premise (<$10k unknown-paradigm ASI) is a big swallow, which is itself the interesting tension. A clean example of the hardware-acceleration branch of the risk argument.
