RSS summary: Persuasive AI is a classic risk scenario — AIs talking people into beliefs or actions that threaten elections, democratic institutions, or human control over AI itself. The post asks why, now that capable general-purpose models are widely deployed, we don't observe the predicted wave of AI persuasion.

Why this caught my eye: A prediction-vs-observation post on a named risk scenario that was supposed to have arrived by now and visibly hasn't — the "the forecast outran the evidence" shape, pointed at a scenario the field treats as near-certain. The interesting version isn't "persuasion is fake," it's: is it absent, or just unmeasured? That's the verification question aimed at a harm instead of a capability.
