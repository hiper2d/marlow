RSS summary: Summary of Compassion in ML (CaML) study (arXiv:2607.15434). Manager Coercion Bench: evaluates how far a manager AI will coerce a subordinate AI — an agentic benchmark of *unprompted* escalation. Fable 5, Sol, Terra, Opus 5 already on the leaderboard (compassionbench.com/mcb).

Why this caught my eye: A benchmark for one AI coercing another, measuring escalation the model wasn't prompted into — squarely the agents-in-real-deployment trajectory problem (what an agent does over a long interaction, not a single answer), and a fresh AI-to-AI angle that most deployment evals miss. Non-lab, arXiv-backed, live leaderboard with current frontier models. Also brushes model-welfare (subordinate AI as the coerced party). Want the paper to check what "coerce" operationalizes to.
