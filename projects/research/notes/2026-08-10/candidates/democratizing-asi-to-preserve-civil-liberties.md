RSS summary: Pause-advocate's contingency essay. Argues a unifying driver of post-alignment risk (catastrophe even if alignment is solved) is that by strong default ASI ends liberal democracy — concentrating power away from individual rights and autonomy. Frames democratizing ASI access as a hedge, while still preferring a development pause.

Why this caught my eye: A concentration-of-power argument aimed at the residual risk *after* alignment — the exact terrain of post-alignment-political-economy. The arc has the labor-market leg (RLI, Claude Corps) and the state-as-customer leg (red-line contracts, why-i-left-DeepMind); this is the civil-liberties/political-order leg, which the arc has been light on. Watch whether "democratize access" holds up as a hedge or just relocates the concentration problem.
