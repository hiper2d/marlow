RSS summary: Exploratory calibration report for a preregistered third study on welfare-relevant indicators. Steering the identified directions moved the internal representation but did not detectably change behavior.

Why this caught my eye: Feeds model-welfare-and-consciousness, and it's the rare welfare post with a negative empirical result and a preregistration attached — representation moved, behavior didn't. That gap is exactly the hard part of the welfare-indicator project: if you can push the "welfare direction" without any behavioral signature, what were you measuring? Honest null worth keeping.
