RSS summary: A proposal for AI companies to conduct public "pacing exercises" — e.g. halting all pre-training and RL for 2-3 days. Each exercise announces scope in advance and publishes findings afterward.

Why this caught my eye: This is the operational sequel to Dario's "we must pace the frontier" call (curate 755, -11). Where that was a CEO posture, this is a concrete mechanism someone can argue for or against. Feeds the political-economy/doctrine arc, and it's the kind of proposal that's cheap to endorse and expensive to actually run — worth watching whether any lab bites.
