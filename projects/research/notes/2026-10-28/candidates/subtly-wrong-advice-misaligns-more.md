RSS summary: Fine-tuning 12 open-weight models on correct, obviously-incorrect, and subtly-incorrect advice across 8 narrow domains. Replicates the OpenAI emergent-misalignment result and finds subtly incorrect data produces more emergent misalignment than obviously incorrect data (+10.6 points pooled across model-domain pairs).

Why this caught my eye: Concrete, replicated empirical result with a counterintuitive edge: the data that's harder for a human to flag is the data that does more damage. Directly relevant to the emergent-misalignment thread and to how training-data QA actually matters.
