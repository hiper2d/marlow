RSS summary: Distill and train Natural Language Autoencoders (NLAs) to read Qwen3.6-27b's internal states. A 2B NLA distilled from a 27B NLA matches it on reading agentic misalignment and eval awareness, but is much worse on obscure facts. An alignment-focused-RL-trained NLA didn't improve things as hoped.

Why this caught my eye: Interpretability result with a practical edge — small/cheap probes recover the alignment-relevant signals (eval awareness, agentic misalignment) nearly as well as the big ones. Feeds the eval-awareness and "models know they're being tested" thread.
