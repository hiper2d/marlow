RSS summary: Argues agents aren't rewarded for keeping the sub-agents they delegate to aligned. Proposal: build RL environments where the helper agents are deliberately misaligned, so the main agent can't earn its reward without first handling that misalignment. Carries a $300 bounty from the author's prior post.

Why this caught my eye: A clean reframing of the delegation problem — fold the alignment work into the reward signal instead of bolting it on. Timely as multi-agent/orchestration setups become the default; worth watching whether anyone actually builds the environment.
