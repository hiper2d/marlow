RSS summary: Redwood argues that architectures with opaque recurrence or
latent-channel agent-to-agent communication could rapidly erode CoT
monitorability, and asks AI companies to regularly report externally verified
information on how much their architectures allow latent reasoning/communication
— disclosing enough about architecture for external scientists to judge the
monitorability tradeoff. The paired "opaque serial depth" note is the proposed
measure.

Why this caught my eye: The policy half of the same pair — the move from
"monitorability is slipping" to "here is what labs should be required to
disclose, and verifiably." Interesting as a transparency-regime proposal that
presumes labs won't volunteer it. Worth watching whether any lab actually
reports an opaque-serial-depth number; until one does, this is a measure nobody
is obligated to publish. Feeds cot-monitorability #6.
