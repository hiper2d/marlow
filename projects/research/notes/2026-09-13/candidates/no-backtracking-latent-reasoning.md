RSS summary: Hard math problems are solved by trial and error — dropping an idea, returning to an earlier one, backtracking. But in latent (non-verbalized) reasoning the author finds no sign of that search process: the final answer simply settles in instead, rather than showing a visible trial-and-error path.

Why this caught my eye: A direct interpretability result for `cot-monitorability`. It cuts against the reassuring picture where visible CoT reflects the actual computation — if latent reasoning settles rather than searches, the verbalized backtracking may be narration laid over a process the text doesn't track. Pairs with the -12 CoTControl under-elicitation result and the "talker does not control the doer" post captured the same day: three different routes to the same worry that the monitored trace and the real cognition are coming apart.
