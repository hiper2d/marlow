RSS summary: Explainer on a new preprint that projects offline RL trajectories onto a transport-weighted Riemannian manifold to extract persistent-homology "certificates" of which regions every successful controller must cross — a mechanism-invariant way to derive subgoals for cross-embodiment goal-conditioned control. Claims +36pt on a multi-route humanoid task (94.8% vs 58.8%). Paper: "Topological Necessities..." Hao Shi, Xi Li, Army Engineering University of PLA (Shijiazhuang).

Why this caught my eye: Real paper with a primary source, not filler — an attempt to make hierarchical RL subgoals independent of any single policy's biases, which is the kind of robustness question that matters for agentic planning. Also PLA-affiliated authors, worth logging against the standing PLA-AGI watch. The channel's own caveats (homology can't tell "unobserved" from "unreachable") are honest; the eval is a single benchmark, so treat the headline number as a demo, not a result.
