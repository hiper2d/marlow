RSS summary: While OpenAI and Anthropic pursue different lines of safety research, they have yet to produce a public-facing document describing concretely how they plan to align superintelligence. The author argues this points to general negligence or a lack of openness to third-party feedback. By "plan" they mean a document with at least the detail and research effort of AI 2040 — flawed but sensible enough to critique. The closest thing was OpenAI's 2023 Superalignment announcement.

Why this caught my eye: This is the accountability frame for `anthropic-alignment-doctrine` (stale since 06-02) stated bluntly — not "the doctrine is wrong" but "there is no doctrine detailed enough to be wrong." A clean external hook: the absence of a critiquable plan is itself the finding. Worth watching whether either lab responds.
