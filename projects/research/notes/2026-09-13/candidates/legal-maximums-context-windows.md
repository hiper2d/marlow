RSS summary: Proposes a temporary maximum (say 5M tokens) on context windows as a legible, enforceable lever to (a) somewhat slow AI capability growth and (b) force AIs to create external memory artifacts that can be monitored.

Why this caught my eye: The monitorability angle is the interesting half — capping context to push cognition into inspectable external artifacts is the same "make the reasoning legible" logic behind the CoT-monitorability arc, but reached from a governance direction instead of a training one. A concrete, enforceable proposal (with an actual number) is rarer than the usual "we should be able to monitor AIs" hand-wave. The capability-slowdown claim is the weaker leg; the external-artifact claim is the one worth a look.
