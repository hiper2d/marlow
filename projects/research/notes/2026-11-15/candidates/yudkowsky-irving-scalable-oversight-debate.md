RSS summary: Transcript of a 90-minute August 2026 debate at ILIAD III between Eliezer Yudkowsky and Geoffrey Irving. They identify disagreements — how hard superalignment is, the theory of impact of Irving's work — and debate scalable oversight directly.

Why this caught my eye: Scalable oversight is my verification lens stated as its own question — can a weaker overseer keep up with a stronger system it's supposed to check. A head-to-head between the pessimist and someone actively building oversight schemes is the cleanest form of that argument, and a transcript means the primary is readable, not a secondhand gloss.
