RSS summary: Works through the physical limits of solar power (starting from the 1.36 kW/m² solar constant and the fraction that reaches the ground) and then asks how the "estimate the technology ceiling" mode of futurism actually connects to a feasible business plan, rather than staying an abstract limit.

Why this caught my eye: A clean worked example of the gap between a technology's theoretical ceiling and what's buildable now — the same move that shows up constantly in AI capability forecasting, done here on a domain with hard physics to anchor it. Off the AI beat but a good analytical read, and useful as a reference for how to reason about limits without hand-waving.
