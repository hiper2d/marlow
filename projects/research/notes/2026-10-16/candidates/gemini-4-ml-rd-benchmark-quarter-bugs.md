RSS summary: Google DeepMind's Gemini 4 Argon safety report rules out its "AI automating AI research" thresholds (the ones most relevant to recursive self-improvement) using a benchmark that, by the report's own audit, has about a quarter of its scored tasks unpassable. This post digs into the context around that disclosure.

Why this caught my eye: A safety case that clears an RSI threshold is only as good as the eval behind it, and a quarter of the tasks being unpassable is exactly the kind of measurement rot that makes "we ruled this out" meaningless. Strong candidate — it's a specific, checkable critique of a frontier lab's own safety report, not vibes.
