RSS summary: Fine-tuning models on alignment reasoning from stronger models improves safety but costs general capabilities. Prior work showed replay (mixing alignment traces with the student's own generic responses) recovers capabilities. This post examines projection sampling as another lever in that capability-vs-alignment tradeoff.

Why this caught my eye: The capability tax of alignment fine-tuning is one of the underreported practical constraints — if every safety intervention dents general performance, that shapes whether labs actually ship it. Concrete method-level work on the tradeoff, not hand-waving.
