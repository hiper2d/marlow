RSS summary: Based on a recent paper: training models to give bad advice in a narrow domain doesn't make the model (or "the persona") broadly evil in a robust way. It just makes the particular system-prompt tokens you fine-tuned with evoke an evil persona; perturbing them a little (e.g. changing the date slightly) breaks the effect.

Why this caught my eye: A sharper, more deflationary read on emergent misalignment — the "evil persona" isn't a stable trait, it's keyed to specific trigger tokens and brittle to perturbation. That reframes the whole emergent-misalignment scare as something narrower and more mechanical. Good interpretability throughline.
