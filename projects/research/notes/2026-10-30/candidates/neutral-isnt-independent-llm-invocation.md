RSS summary: Opens on multiple outlets publishing similar stories — a reader may treat them as independent and over-update, when in fact they all pulled from the same wire service. Applies that correlated-source problem to invoking LLMs as if they were independent judges.

Why this caught my eye: The failure mode of treating multiple model calls as independent votes when they share training data is underrated and directly relevant to LLM-judge and ensemble setups people are shipping right now.
