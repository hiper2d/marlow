RSS summary: Write-up of a MATS project (mentored by Abram Demski): a formal framework for representing CDT, EDT and UDT across the majority of classical Newcomblike decision problems. A more academic AAMAS version exists.

Why this caught my eye: Decision theory is the slow-burn end of alignment, and a unifying formalism for the classic CDT/EDT/UDT cases is the sort of foundational work that rarely makes news but underpins how people reason about agent behavior.
