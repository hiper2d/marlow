RSS summary: A Forethought piece asking whether a first mover could deny others access to space, or raise the cost of entry, to cement a lead. Defines soft lock-out (capturing scarce orbital/lunar assets — e.g. occupying orbital bands with data centres) and hard lock-out, estimating a ~$49–73B premium on a ~$1T build.

Why this caught my eye: The orbital-data-centre angle ties this to the AI compute race rather than leaving it as abstract space policy — the "entrench a lead by owning the scarce physical substrate" logic is the same argument people make about frontier compute, now pointed at orbit.
