RSS summary: Proposes a general way to define and decode the internal representations transformers use for prediction, framed around "generalized belief states." Asks whether representation can be considered without reference to an external world model, and whether such states can be interpreted and manipulated.

Why this caught my eye: this is the computational-mechanics line of interp (belief-state geometry), which has been one of the more rigorous and less hand-wavy corners of the field. A generalization attempt here is a real technical contribution, not a vibes post — good counterweight to the SAE pessimism also surfacing today.
