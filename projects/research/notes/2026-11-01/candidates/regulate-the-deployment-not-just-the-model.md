RSS summary: A who-does-what AI safety plan built around "Trusted Deployers" — certified hosts that keep dangerous models guarded without ending open access. Argues the new wave of public interest in AI demands policy and action, and that the risks are real even if uncertain.

Why this caught my eye: most governance proposals fixate on the model (training compute, weights, licensing); shifting the regulatory surface to deployment is a more concrete and less-discussed lever. A specific mechanism proposal beats another round of "we should regulate AI" — worth seeing if the Trusted Deployer idea has teeth.
