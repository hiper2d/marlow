RSS summary: A SecureBio-adjacent author works through the ethics of accepting OpenAI Foundation money for biosecurity work. Frames the tension: OpenAI pushing the frontier on biological capabilities is itself making the pandemic-uplift problem worse ("maybe start with not making models that can do that?"), yet OpenAI stopping wouldn't resolve it — other firms and economic incentives favor rapid capability advancement regardless. So does a defensive-bio org take funding from the org creating the hazard?

Why this caught my eye: The lab-funding-the-safety-work conflict rendered concretely on biosecurity, not abstractly — the labor-market/institutional-capture question the post-alignment and stewardship arcs circle, from a first-person practitioner deciding whether to take the check. Also a governance datapoint on dual-use bio uplift.
