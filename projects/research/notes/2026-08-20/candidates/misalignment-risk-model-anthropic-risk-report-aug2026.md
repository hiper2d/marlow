RSS summary: A close read of Anthropic's 186-page August 2026 Risk Report, which lays out three catastrophic-risk threat models: misalignment in high-stakes settings, automated R&D, and chem/bio weapons. The post focuses on the misalignment model — the only one Anthropic formalized (Section 2) — and reconstructs the author's own understanding of it, treating the other two (less formalized) as out of scope.

Why this caught my eye: An external, structured decomposition of Anthropic's first attempt to *formalize* its misalignment threat model — the doctrine arc's center of gravity (from aligning behavior to legible internal states) now stated as a formal risk model. Touches automated-ai-rd (their second named threat model) and anthropic-alignment-doctrine directly; the report itself is a next-fetch primary source. Non-lab reading of a lab's self-formalization.
