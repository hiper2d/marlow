RSS summary: LLMs express functional welfare states (flourishing, distress), but most studies read those off the model's *self-report*, which may not be faithful. This study builds a valence metric by probing internal activations in the J-space, validates it, then compares it to self-reports. Divergence between the internal metric and self-report varies by model.

Why this caught my eye: This is the J-space anchor (Anthropic's "global workspace") turned into a *welfare-measurement* tool by an outside researcher — internal-state valence vs stated valence, with a say/do gap between them. Direct feed to model-welfare-and-consciousness, and it rhymes hard with the emotion-vector result (internal state that steers behavior while the surface reads differently). Non-lab replication/extension of a lab construct is exactly the diversity that arc wants.
