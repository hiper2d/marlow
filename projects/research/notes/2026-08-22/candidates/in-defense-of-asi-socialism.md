RSS summary: Author (self-described abundance-center-left liberal, not a socialist) argues that if we don't halt AI before ASI, we should strip all humans of long-term control of capital and cede it to the state just before ASI, then hand control to the ASI under an equal-distribution constitution. Frames it as a pre-ASI ownership question, not a general economic program.

Why this caught my eye: A concrete (if radical) proposal on the pre-ASI capital-ownership question that post-alignment-political-economy keeps circling — who holds capital when comparative advantage collapses. Sits opposite Clark's markets-persist bet and the red-line/contract-language anchors. Opinion piece, not evidence, but it's the ownership-side fork stated bluntly enough to argue with.
