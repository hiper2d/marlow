RSS summary: Preprint (arXiv:2608.12447, code + supplementary). The directions nearest a model's prediction decide what kind of answer you get; the next ones out decide where it goes ~5 tokens later. Headline finding (#8): temporal stratification of output behavior in residual-stream geometry — the geometry is "privileged" by proximity to the model's own prediction.

Why this caught my eye: A non-lab interp result about residual-stream geometry that claims the stream is *layered by prediction-proximity and by future-time-horizon*. If it holds, that's a structural handle on where a model's near-term vs several-tokens-out behavior lives — relevant to the horizon-length watch cluster and to cot-monitorability's question of what's actually legible in internals. Judge the paper, not the post.
