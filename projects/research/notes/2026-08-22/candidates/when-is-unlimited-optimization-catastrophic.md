RSS summary: Research from Dovetail Research (Cymbalista, Harwood, Faustino), funded by ARIA (MSAI-SE01-P005), with an arXiv paper. Revisits the "human value is fragile" justification for AI danger — that modifying our values and then heavily optimizing the world for the modification tends toward a valueless world — and asks when unlimited optimization is actually catastrophic vs when it isn't.

Why this caught my eye: A formal, funded attempt to put conditions on the fragility-of-value argument rather than assume it — the kind of "is the load-bearing intuition actually true" work that alignment-target-definitions collects. If it delimits *when* heavy optimization destroys value, that's a real handle on a claim usually asserted whole. Judge the paper; the post is the on-ramp.
