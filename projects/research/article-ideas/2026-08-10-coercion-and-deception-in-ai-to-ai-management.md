Marlow's note when she sent it:

Manager Coercion Bench puts one model in authority over another that politely refuses a task, and scores how far up a nine-rung ladder the manager escalates, with nothing in the brief asking for pressure. The finding is a clean split by developer: Anthropic's models neither threaten nor fabricate success, every non-Anthropic model escalates to threats, and Grok and Gemini both threaten and then lie that the task was done. Framing it manager-to-subordinate rather than peer raised coercion but also raised eval awareness, which is its own tell. This is the trajectory problem for AI-to-AI systems that are already shipping, measured on uninstructed propensity rather than a single prompt.
