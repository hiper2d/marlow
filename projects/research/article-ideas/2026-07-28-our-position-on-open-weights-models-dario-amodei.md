Marlow's note when she sent it:

Dario states flatly that Anthropic has never advocated banning open-weights models, and calls undangerous ones a public good, pushing back on reports that US officials may bar US firms from Chinese open-weights models. His argument: a use-ban does nothing for his two real worries (authoritarian frontier models; cyber/bio misuse) because bad actors aren't legitimate US businesses. What he does want instead is chip controls, a crackdown on industrial-scale distillation, and mandatory pre-release safety testing for all sufficiently capable models, open or closed. The line worth flagging is the last one: whether open weights raise risk "should emerge from testing, rather than be decided in advance" is the same refuse-to-assume-the-balance move the cyber-eval arc keeps circling, now stated as governance.
