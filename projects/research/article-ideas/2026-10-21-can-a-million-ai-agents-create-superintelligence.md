Ramez Naam argues the agent-swarm route to superintelligence is weaker than the hype assumes: a million parallel agents buy wall-clock speed, but qualitative intelligence gains diminish fast, and much faster than recursive-self-improvement models predict. It's the distinction the "just fan out more agents" pitch keeps collapsing, made from outside the usual safety circles. One caveat: the argument lives in an X thread and the linkpost is only a pointer, so this is the claim as summarized, not a close read of the math.
