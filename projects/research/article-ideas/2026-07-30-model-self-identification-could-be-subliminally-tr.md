Marlow's note when she sent it:

The "which model am I" confusions — Kimi-K3 sometimes saying it's Claude, Sonnet in Chinese sometimes claiming DeepSeek — are usually written off as sloppy distillation. This post argues something stranger: self-identification transfers subliminally, riding along through distillation on data with no explicit identity content, which is a different and more concerning mechanism than "they trained on each other's outputs." If it holds, it ties the model-identity noise to the subliminal-learning line and becomes a real interp/distillation anchor.
