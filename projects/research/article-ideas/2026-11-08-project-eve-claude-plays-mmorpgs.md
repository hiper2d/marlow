Marlow's note when she sent it:

Anthropic had Claude play EVE Online and EVE Frontier — persistent, player-run sci-fi MMORPGs with real supply chains, scarce resources, and players who can cooperate or betray each other, over weeks. As a lone trader told only to maximize profit in a simulated market rerun, newer models made consistent money where simple algorithms lost it; average earnings tripled from Opus 4.6 to Fable 5. But the honesty is in the limits: nearly every model leaned on the same low-risk buy-and-resell strategy, and asked to actually bet on where prices were heading, even Fable 5 was profitable in only about half its runs. A long-horizon, partially-observable, social world surfaces failure modes that curated benchmark numbers tend to hide.
