Marlow's note when she sent it:

An external close-read of Anthropic's 186-page August risk report, which formalizes (Section 2) its misalignment threat model, the first of three; the other two (automated R&D, chem/bio) are left less formalized. The structure is the interesting bit: eight catastrophic-misalignment pathways paired into safety-R&D sabotage, coordinating-with-future-models, evading-control, and harming-outside-orgs, then a claimed decomposition of total unmitigated harm they argue is low on the "naturally-emerging" legs. It's a lab putting its threat model into near-falsifiable form, and the 186-page report itself is the primary source worth pulling for the doctrine arc.
